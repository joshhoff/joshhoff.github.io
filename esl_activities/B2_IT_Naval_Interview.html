Master's Program Interview Practice for Naval Engineering Students

Self-Presentation

Time: 2:00

You have a maximum of 2 minutes to introduce yourself, explain your motivation for applying to the Master's program, and discuss your mobility availability. Be creative!

Tips:

  • Start with a brief personal introduction
  • Highlight your naval engineering background
  • Connect your experience to the program
  • Mention specific reasons for your interest
  • Address your availability for mobility clearly
  • Consider using a metaphor related to naval engineering

Business Case Discussion

In this section, you'll reason in a group on topics related to the business, applying your academic skills and engaging with other participants.

Preparation Tips:

  • Research current trends in maritime industry
  • Prepare to discuss sustainability in naval engineering
  • Consider cost-efficiency in shipbuilding
  • Be ready to analyze case studies
  • Practice articulating technical concepts clearly

Sample Business Case Topics

Sustainable Shipbuilding Practices

Discuss innovative approaches to reduce environmental impact in modern shipbuilding.

Maritime Automation Technologies

Analyze the cost-benefit of implementing automation in vessel operations.

Alternative Propulsion Systems

Evaluate the feasibility of hydrogen, electric, and hybrid propulsion for commercial vessels.

Individual Interviews

In this section, you'll have the opportunity to speak with representatives from Scuola Mattei for technical discussions and with a Recruiter for an aptitude and motivational interview.

Technical Interview Tips:

  • Review fundamental naval engineering concepts
  • Prepare to discuss your technical projects
  • Be ready to solve engineering problems
  • Practice explaining complex concepts simply
  • Consider bringing portfolio examples

Motivational Interview Tips:

  • Reflect on your career goals
  • Prepare stories about overcoming challenges
  • Consider your fit with the program's values
  • Think about your unique strengths
  • Research the program thoroughly

Common Interview Questions

Technical Questions

  • • Explain the principles of ship stability and buoyancy.
  • • How would you approach optimizing a hull design for fuel efficiency?
  • • Describe a technical challenge you faced in a naval engineering project and how you solved it.
  • • What do you consider the most significant innovation in maritime engineering in recent years?

Motivational Questions

  • • Why have you chosen to pursue further education in this specific field?
  • • How do you see this Master's program contributing to your career goals?
  • • Describe a situation where you demonstrated leadership in a team project.
  • • What aspects of international mobility are you most excited about?
