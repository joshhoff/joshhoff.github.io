Present Perfect Tense

Presente Perfecto en Inglés

What is the Present Perfect?

The Present Perfect tense connects the past with the present. It describes actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past or actions that started in the past and continue to the present.

FORMULA: Subject + have/has + past participle
I have visited Paris.
He visitado París.
She has lived here for 5 years.
Ella ha vivido aquí por 5 años.
We have finished our homework.
Hemos terminado nuestra tarea.
They have never seen snow.
Nunca han visto nieve.

When to Use Present Perfect

Unfinished time periods:
I have read 3 books this month.
Períodos de tiempo no terminados:
He leído 3 libros este mes.
Life experiences:
Have you ever been to Japan?
Experiencias de vida:
¿Has estado alguna vez en Japón?
Recent actions with present results:
I have lost my keys.
Acciones recientes con resultados presentes:
He perdido mis llaves.
Actions continuing to present:
She has worked here since 2020.
Acciones que continúan al presente:
Ella ha trabajado aquí desde 2020.
