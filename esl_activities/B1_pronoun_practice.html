Quick Reference Guide

Subject
Performs the action
I, you, he, she, we, they
Object
Receives the action
me, you, him, her, us, them
Reflexive
Refers back to subject
myself, yourself, himself
Possessive
Shows ownership
mine, yours, his, hers
Indefinite
Refers to unknown
someone, everyone, nothing
