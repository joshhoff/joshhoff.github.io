What is Connected Speech?

Connected speech refers to the natural way words flow together in spoken language, often resulting in sounds that differ from when words are pronounced in isolation. Native speakers unconsciously modifyTo change something slightly, especially to improve it sounds between words to create smoother, more efficient speech patterns.
