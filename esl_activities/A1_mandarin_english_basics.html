Scenario 1: Meeting Someone New

You are at a coffee shop and meet someone new. Practice introducing yourself and asking basic questions.


Key phrases to use:
  • Hi, I'm...
  • What's your name?
  • Where are you from?
  • Nice to meet you!

Scenario 2: Ordering Food

You are at a restaurant and want to order food. Practice polite ordering and asking questions about the menu.


Key phrases to use:
  • I'd like to order...
  • What do you recommend?
  • Can I have the check, please?
  • Thank you very much!

Scenario 3: Asking for Directions

You are lost and need to ask someone for directions to the nearest bank.


Key phrases to use:
  • Excuse me, can you help me?
  • Where is the nearest...?
  • How do I get there?
  • Thank you for your help!
