Online Tutoring Phrases

Essential phrases for online English lessons and communication with tutors.

Hello / Hola

English: Hello, good morning!

Spanish: ¡Hola, buenos días!

How are you? / ¿Cómo estás?

English: How are you today?

Spanish: ¿Cómo estás hoy?

Repeat please / Repite por favor

English: Can you repeat that, please?

Spanish: ¿Puedes repetir eso, por favor?

Speak slowly / Habla despacio

English: Please speak more slowly

Spanish: Por favor habla más despacio

I have a question / Tengo una pregunta

English: I have a question about this

Spanish: Tengo una pregunta sobre esto

I don't understand / No entiendo

English: I don't understand this word

Spanish: No entiendo esta palabra
