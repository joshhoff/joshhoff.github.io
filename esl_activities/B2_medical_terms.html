Conversation Practice Prompts

🏥 At the Emergency Room
You arrive at the emergency room with severe chest pain. Describe your symptoms to the triage nurse, including when the pain started, its intensity, and any other symptoms you're experiencing.
💊 Discussing Medication
Your doctor has prescribed new medication. Ask about potential side effects, dosage instructions, and whether it interacts with your current medications.
🔬 Understanding Test Results
You've received blood test results showing elevated cholesterol. Discuss with your doctor what this means, lifestyle changes you should make, and whether medication is necessary.
🏃 Post-Surgery Follow-up
You're at a follow-up appointment after knee surgery. Describe your recovery progress, any complications, and ask about when you can resume normal activities.
🤒 Calling in Sick
Call your workplace to explain you can't come in due to illness. Describe your symptoms professionally and mention you've seen a doctor.
