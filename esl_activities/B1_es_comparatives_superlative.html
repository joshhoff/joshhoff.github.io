📖 Understanding Comparatives & Superlatives

🔵 COMPARATIVE ADJECTIVES

Used to compare TWO things

A is [comparative] than B

📏 Short Adjectives + -er

Rule: Add -er to the end

• tall → taller

• fast → faster

• big → bigger (double consonant)

🐘 bigger than 🐭

"The elephant is bigger than the mouse"

📚 Long Adjectives: more + adjective

Rule: Use "more" before the adjective

• beautiful → more beautiful

• expensive → more expensive

• interesting → more interesting

💎 more expensive than 🪨

"Diamonds are more expensive than rocks"

🟢 SUPERLATIVE ADJECTIVES

Used to compare THREE OR MORE things

A is THE [superlative] of all

📏 Short Adjectives + -est

Rule: Add -est to the end

• tall → tallest

• fast → fastest

• big → biggest (double consonant)

🦒

"The giraffe is the tallest animal"

📚 Long Adjectives: most + adjective

Rule: Use "most" before the adjective

• beautiful → the most beautiful

• expensive → the most expensive

• interesting → the most interesting

🌅

"Sunsets are the most beautiful sight"

⚠️ IRREGULAR ADJECTIVES

These adjectives don't follow the regular rules - you must memorize them!

GOOD

😊 good
😄 better
🤩 best

BAD

😐 bad
😞 worse
😫 worst

FAR

🏠 far
🏘️ farther
🌍 farthest
