📚 Quick Overview

Simple Present

For habits, facts, and routines

I work every day

Present Continuous

For actions happening now

I am working now

Simple Present Tense

When to use:

  • • Daily routines and habits
  • • General facts and truths
  • • Permanent situations

Formation:

Positive: I/You/We/They + verb

Positive: He/She/It + verb + s

Negative: Subject + don't/doesn't + verb

Question: Do/Does + subject + verb?

Examples:

I work in an office.

(Daily routine)

She speaks three languages.

(Permanent ability)

The sun rises in the east.

(General fact)

Present Continuous Tense

When to use:

  • • Actions happening right now
  • • Temporary situations
  • • Future plans (with time expressions)

Formation:

Positive: Subject + am/is/are + verb + ing

Negative: Subject + am/is/are + not + verb + ing

Question: Am/Is/Are + subject + verb + ing?

Examples:

I am working right now.

(Happening now)

She is studying abroad this year.

(Temporary situation)

We are meeting tomorrow at 3 PM.

(Future plan)

Key Differences

Simple Present

I drink coffee every morning.

(Regular habit)

She works at a bank.

(Permanent job)

Present Continuous

I am drinking coffee right now.

(Happening now)

She is working on a special project.

(Temporary activity)

Conversation Practice

Conversation Prompts

Daily Routines

• What do you usually do in the morning?

• How often do you exercise?

• What time do you typically go to bed?

What's Happening Now?

• What are you doing right now?

• What is your family doing today?

• What are you working on this week?

Mixed Practice

• Do you like your job? What are you doing at work these days?

• Where do you live? Are you planning to move?

• What do you do for fun? What are you doing this weekend?

Role Play Scenarios

🏢 At the Office

You're talking to a colleague about work:

A: What do you usually do on Mondays?

B: I usually have team meetings. What are you working on today?

A: I'm preparing a presentation for tomorrow...

☕ At a Café

You meet a friend for coffee:

A: How are things going? What are you up to these days?

B: Pretty good! I'm taking a cooking class this month...

📱 Phone Call

You're calling a friend:

A: Hi! What are you doing right now?

B: I'm just watching TV. Do you want to come over?
