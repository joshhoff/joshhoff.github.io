Conversation Practice

Practice these conversation prompts with a partner or write your responses. Pay attention to the correct use of prepositions.

Situation: Drone Photography Class

Teacher: Where should we fly the drone to get the best photos?

You: We should fly it the sky above the lake.

Teacher: What time will we meet for the class?

You: We will meet 3 o'clock the park entrance.

Teacher: Where is the memory card for the drone?

You: The memory card is the drone's camera.

Answers: in, at, at, in

Situation: Drone Delivery Service

Practice this conversation with a partner:

Customer: Where is my package now?

You (Service Rep): Your package is currently on a delivery drone.

Customer: When will it arrive?

You: It will arrive at your address in approximately 30 minutes.

Customer: Where should I collect the package?

You: The drone will land on your driveway and you can collect the package at that location.

Notice how "in", "at", and "on" are used in this conversation.

Drone Technology Vocabulary

Drone Parts

  • Propeller: The spinning blades on a drone
  • Camera: Usually mounted on the bottom of a drone
  • Battery: Stored in the drone body
  • Controller: The device in your hands
  • Sensors: Located on various parts of the drone

Drone Actions

  • Hover: To stay in one position in the air
  • Land: To come down on a surface
  • Take off: To rise from a surface into the air
  • Navigate: To fly through an area
  • Record: To capture video with the drone camera

Locations & Positions

  • The drone flies in the sky
  • The pilot stands at the control station
  • The camera is mounted on the drone
  • We operate drones in open spaces
  • The drone hovers at a height of 50 meters

Time Expressions

  • The drone will arrive at 3 o'clock
  • We fly drones in the morning
  • The battery lasts for 30 minutes
  • The drone show is on Saturday
  • We'll meet at noon at the park
