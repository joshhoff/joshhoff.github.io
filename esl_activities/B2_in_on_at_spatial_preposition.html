Master spatial prepositions: IN, ON, AT

🎯 Understanding Spatial Prepositions

Prepositions show the relationship between objects in space. Let's learn when to use IN, ON, and AT!

📦 IN - Inside or enclosed spaces

Use "IN" when something is inside or enclosed by something else.

🏠 Buildings & Rooms:
• The book is in the library
• She's in the kitchen
• We live in New York
📦 Containers:
• The milk is in the fridge
• Put the letter in the envelope
• The keys are in my bag
🌍 Large Areas:
• Fish swim in the ocean
• Birds fly in the sky
• We walked in the park
📋 ON - Surfaces and contact

Use "ON" when something is touching a surface or is attached to something.

🪑 Surfaces:
• The cup is on the table
• Hang the picture on the wall
• She sits on the chair
🚌 Transportation:
• We traveled on the bus
• He's on the train
• They sailed on the ship
📍 Specific Locations:
• The store is on Main Street
• We live on the second floor
• Meet me on the corner
📍 AT - Specific points and locations

Use "AT" for specific points, addresses, and exact locations.

🏢 Specific Places:
• Meet me at the library
• She works at the hospital
• We're at home
🏠 Addresses:
• I live at 123 Oak Street
• The party is at Sarah's house
• Meet at the bus stop
🎯 Exact Points:
• Stand at the door
• Wait at the traffic light
• She's at the front desk

💬 Conversation Practice

Use these prompts to practice speaking with prepositions. Try to answer each question using IN, ON, or AT correctly!

🏠 Describing Your Home
  • Where do you keep your books? (Use "in")
  • What's hanging on your bedroom wall? (Use "on")
  • Where do you usually eat dinner at home? (Use "at")
  • What room are you in right now?
🌆 Around the City
  • Where do you go to buy groceries? (Use "at")
  • What street is your favorite restaurant on? (Use "on")
  • What do you like to do in the park? (Use "in")
  • Where do you wait for the bus?
🎒 Daily Activities
  • Where do you put your phone when you sleep? (Use "on" or "in")
  • Where do you usually study? (Use "at" or "in")
  • What do you carry in your bag?
  • Where do you meet your friends?
🚗 Transportation & Travel
  • How do you get to work or school? (on the bus, in a car, etc.)
  • Where do you sit when you're on public transportation?
  • What's the best place to visit in your city?
  • Where do you like to go on weekends?

💡 Speaking Tips

Practice out loud! Try recording yourself answering these questions, or practice with a friend. Pay attention to which preposition feels natural in each context.

Remember: IN = inside/enclosed, ON = surface/contact, AT = specific point/location
