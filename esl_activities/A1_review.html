Common Verbs (Verbos Comunes)

Why Learn Clothing Vocabulary?

Learning clothing vocabulary in English helps you:

  • Go shopping for clothes confidently
  • Describe what you or others are wearing
  • Understand sizes, colors, and styles
  • Ask for help in clothing stores

Real-Life Situations:

"I need a new shirt for work."

"Do you have these jeans in size 32?"

"I like your dress! Where did you buy it?"

Cultural Note:

In English-speaking countries, clothing sizes may be different from what you're used to. Learning to ask about sizes and trying clothes on is an important skill!

Why Learn Numbers in English?

Numbers are essential in everyday English for:

  • Understanding prices when shopping
  • Telling time and making appointments
  • Sharing your phone number or address
  • Discussing quantities and measurements

Real-Life Situations:

"The shirt costs twenty dollars."

"My appointment is at three thirty."

"I need two tickets, please."

Cultural Note:

In English, commas and periods in numbers are used differently than in Spanish. For example: 1,000.50 in English is 1.000,50 in Spanish.

Why Learn Common Verbs?

Common verbs are the building blocks of conversation:

  • Describe your daily activities
  • Express needs, wants, and preferences
  • Ask questions and request information
  • Tell stories about past experiences

Real-Life Situations:

"I work at a restaurant."

"She likes to read books."

"Can you help me, please?"

Cultural Note:

In English, the verb form doesn't change as much as in Spanish, but word order is very important for making questions and statements.

Why Learn Animal Vocabulary?

Learning animal vocabulary helps you:

  • Talk about pets and wildlife
  • Understand nature documentaries
  • Discuss animal-related topics
  • Use common animal idioms and expressions

Real-Life Situations:

"I have a dog named Max."

"We saw elephants at the zoo."

"Is that a bird or a bat?"

Cultural Note:

In English-speaking countries, pets are often considered part of the family, and people frequently talk about their pets in conversation.
