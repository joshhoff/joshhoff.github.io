📖 Subject vs Object Pronouns

🇬🇧 English Pronoun System

Person Subject Pronoun Object Pronoun Example
1st Singular I me I see him. He sees me.
2nd Singular you you You help her. She helps you.
3rd Singular (M) he him He calls me. I call him.
3rd Singular (F) she her She knows us. We know her.
3rd Singular (N) it it It works. I fix it.
1st Plural we us We visit them. They visit us.
3rd Plural they them They like pizza. Pizza likes them! 😄

💡 Key Rules

  • Subject pronouns do the action: "I eat pizza"
  • Object pronouns receive the action: "Pizza loves me"
  • After prepositions, use object pronouns: "with him", "for us"
  • In compound subjects/objects: "John and I" (not "John and me")

🔄 Italian vs English Comparison

🇮🇹 Differenze Principali

In italiano: I pronomi soggetto spesso si omettono perché il verbo indica già la persona.

In inglese: I pronomi soggetto sono SEMPRE necessari!

Italiano English Subject English Object Esempio
io / (omesso) I me (Io) parlo → I speak
tu / (omesso) you you (Tu) parli → You speak
lui / egli he him Lui parla → He speaks
lei / ella she her Lei parla → She speaks
esso/essa it it Funziona → It works
noi / (omesso) we us (Noi) parliamo → We speak
loro / essi they them Loro parlano → They speak

⚠️ Errori Comuni per Italiani

❌ Errore: "Is raining" (come in italiano: "Piove")

✅ Corretto: "It is raining"


❌ Errore: "Me and John went" (influenza italiana)

✅ Corretto: "John and I went"


❌ Errore: "Give to I the book" (ordine italiano)

✅ Corretto: "Give me the book"

🎯 Trucchi per Ricordare

  • Soggetto = Chi fa l'azione: "I, you, he, she, it, we, they"
  • Oggetto = Chi riceve l'azione: "me, you, him, her, it, us, them"
  • Dopo preposizioni: sempre oggetto! "with me", "for him", "to us"
  • Test rapido: Se puoi dire "io" in italiano → "I". Se puoi dire "me" → "me"
