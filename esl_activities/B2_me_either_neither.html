Me Either vs Me Neither

Master the correct usage in everyday conversations

🎯 Key Rules to Remember

"Me neither" - Use with negative statements
Example: "I don't like coffee." → "Me neither!"
"Me either" - Use with negative statements (informal)
Example: "I can't swim." → "Me either!"
Important: Both are used to agree with negative statements. "Me neither" is more grammatically correct, while "Me either" is common in casual speech.
Never use with positive statements!
Wrong: "I love pizza." → "Me neither!"
Right: "I love pizza." → "Me too!"
