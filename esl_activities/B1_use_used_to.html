What does "used to" mean?

We use "used to" to talk about things that happened regularly in the past but don't happen now.

"I used to play with dolls, but now I play video games." 🎮

⚡ Structure: Subject + used to + base verb → "She used to live in Paris."

❓

Negatives & Questions

Negative: Subject + didn't use to + base verb

"I didn't use to like broccoli." 🥦

Question: Did + subject + use to + base verb?

"Did you use to have a pet?" 🐶

🚨 Watch out! In negatives and questions, we write "use to" (no d!) because "did" already shows past tense.

⚖️

"use" vs "used to" — What's the difference?

✅ "use" (present)

To do something with a tool or thing right now or generally.

"I use a pencil to draw." ✏️

🕰️ "used to" (past habit)

Something you did before but not anymore.

"I used to draw every day." 🎨

💡

More Examples

  • 🏫 "We used to have a different teacher last year."
  • 🍦 "She didn't use to like ice cream. Now she loves it!"
  • 🚲 "Did you use to ride your bike to school?"
  • 📱 "I use my phone to listen to music." (now — present!)
