Article Rules Reminder:

a - Use before singular countable nouns that start with a consonant sound (a drone, a business)

an - Use before singular countable nouns that start with a vowel sound (an operator, an image)

the - Use for specific things that both speaker and listener know about (the drone we bought yesterday)

∅ (no article) - Often used with plural or uncountable nouns when speaking generally (drones are useful, equipment is expensive)
