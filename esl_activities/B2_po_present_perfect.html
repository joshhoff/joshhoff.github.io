📚 What is Present Perfect?

Structure:

have/has + past participle

I have eaten / She has worked

Key Uses:

  • Actions completed at unspecified time
  • Life experiences
  • Actions continuing to present
  • Recent actions with present relevance

🇵🇱 Polish Perspective

No Direct Equivalent!

Polish uses different tenses and aspects to express these meanings.

Common Polish Translations:

  • Czas przeszły dokonany (perfective past)
  • Czas teraźniejszy (present tense)
  • Już/jeszcze + past tense
