Welcome to Question Master!

Learning to form questions in English is an important skill. There are two main types of questions:

Yes/No Questions

These questions can be answered with "yes" or "no".

Do you like apples?

Do + you + like + apples + ?

Is he tired?

Is + he + tired + ?

Wh- Questions

These questions ask for specific information and start with words like What, Where, When, Why, Who, How.

What do you eat?

What + do + you + eat + ?

Where does she live?

Where + does + she + live + ?
