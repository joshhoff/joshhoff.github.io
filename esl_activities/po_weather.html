Advanced Weather Vocabulary

Drizzle

Light rain falling in very fine drops

Polish: mżawka

Example: "It's just a light drizzle, we don't need umbrellas."

Downpour

A heavy, sudden rainfall

Polish: ulewa

Example: "We got caught in a downpour and were soaked within seconds."

Muggy

Hot and humid weather that feels uncomfortable

Polish: duszny, parny

Example: "It's so muggy today, I can hardly breathe outside."

Sleet

A mixture of rain and snow or hail

Polish: deszcz ze śniegiem

Example: "The sleet made the roads very slippery."

Starting a conversation about weather

  • Lovely weather we're having, isn't it?
  • Can you believe this weather?
  • What's the forecast for tomorrow?

Practice dialogue

Person A: How's the weather looking for the weekend?

Person B: I heard it's going to be overcast on Saturday, but it should clear up by Sunday.

Person A: That's good to hear. I was planning a picnic on Sunday.

Person B: Just keep an eye on the forecast. Sometimes they predict scattered showers this time of year.

overcast - pochmurno (cloudy with no visible sun)

picnic - piknik (outdoor meal)

scattered showers - przelotne opady (rain that occurs intermittently)
