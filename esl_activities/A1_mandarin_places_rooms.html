Conversation Practice

对话练习

Describing Your Home
描述你的家

Practice saying:

"My house has three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchen, and a living room."

Try to describe: What rooms are in your home? Which room is your favorite and why?

练习说：

"我的房子有三间卧室、两间浴室、一间厨房和一间客厅。"

试着描述： 你家有什么房间？你最喜欢哪个房间，为什么？

Asking for Directions
问路

Practice asking:

"Excuse me, where is the nearest hospital?"

"How do I get to the library?"

Practice answering: "The bank is next to the supermarket."

练习问：

"请问，最近的医院在哪里？"

"我怎么去图书馆？"

练习回答： "银行在超市旁边。"

Daily Activities
日常活动

Practice saying:

"I cook in the kitchen every morning."

"I go to the library on weekends."

Discussion: What do you do in each room of your house? What places in town do you visit regularly?

练习说：

"我每天早上在厨房做饭。"

"我周末去图书馆。"

讨论： 你在家里的每个房间做什么？你经常去城里的什么地方？

Role Play: At the Store
角色扮演：在商店

Scenario: You need to buy groceries and ask someone for directions to the supermarket.

Practice dialogue:

A: "Excuse me, is there a supermarket nearby?"

B: "Yes, there's one next to the post office."

A: "Thank you! How far is it?"

B: "It's about 5 minutes walking."

场景： 你需要买杂货，向别人问去超市的路。

练习对话：

A: "请问，附近有超市吗？"

B: "有，邮局旁边有一个。"

A: "谢谢！有多远？"

B: "走路大约5分钟。"
