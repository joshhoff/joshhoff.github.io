Prepositions of Time

Prepositions of time tell us when something happens. They help us understand the relationship between events and time. The most common prepositions of time are:

At

Used for specific times

In

Used for months, years, seasons, longer periods

On

Used for days and dates

Other Time Prepositions

Before, after, during, until, since, for, by, from...to

Examples of Time Prepositions

Here are some examples of how to use prepositions of time correctly:

At

  • I have a meeting at 3 o'clock.
  • She wakes up at dawn.
  • We celebrate at midnight.
  • The store closes at noon.

In

  • I was born in May.
  • They got married in 2019.
  • We go skiing in winter.
  • She'll graduate in the morning.

On

  • The meeting is on Monday.
  • My birthday is on July 21st.
  • We have class on weekdays.
  • The parade is on Independence Day.

Other Prepositions

  • I'll see you after dinner.
  • She's been studying since morning.
  • The movie lasted for two hours.
  • Please arrive before 9 AM.

Speaking Practice

Practice using prepositions of time in conversation. Click on a prompt and prepare a response using the correct prepositions.

Daily Routine

Describe your daily routine using time prepositions (at, in, on).

Example: I wake up at 7 AM in the morning. On weekdays, I have breakfast at 7:30 AM. I work from 9 AM until 5 PM.

Weekend Plans

Talk about your plans for the upcoming weekend using time prepositions.

Example: On Saturday, I'm meeting friends at noon. In the evening, we're going to a concert that starts at 8 PM and lasts for two hours.

Important Events

Describe important events in your life using time prepositions.

Example: I graduated in 2018. Since then, I've been working as a teacher. I got married on June 15th at sunset.

Future Goals

Talk about your future goals and when you plan to achieve them.

Example: In the next five years, I want to start my own business. By the end of this year, I plan to finish my course. I hope to travel abroad during summer.

Speaking Practice Tips

  • Record yourself speaking to practice pronunciation
  • Try to use at least 3-4 different prepositions in each response
  • Practice with a partner if possible
  • Focus on using the correct preposition for each time expression

Prepositions of Place

Prepositions of place tell us where something is located. They help us understand the position or location of objects in relation to each other. The most common prepositions of place are:

In

Used when something is inside or within boundaries

On

Used when something is touching a surface

At

Used for specific locations or points

Other Place Prepositions

Under, over, above, below, between, behind, in front of, next to, beside, among, across from

Examples of Place Prepositions

Here are some examples of how to use prepositions of place correctly:

In

  • The book is in the drawer.
  • She lives in London.
  • The fish swim in the aquarium.
  • There's milk in the refrigerator.

On

  • The laptop is on the desk.
  • The painting hangs on the wall.
  • The cat is sleeping on the sofa.
  • There's a stain on your shirt.

At

  • I'll meet you at the entrance.
  • She's waiting at the bus stop.
  • We're at the airport.
  • The dog is sitting at your feet.

Other Prepositions

  • The ball rolled under the table.
  • The helicopter flew over the city.
  • The cat is hiding behind the curtain.
  • The store is across from the bank.

Speaking Practice

Practice using prepositions of place in conversation. Click on a prompt and prepare a response using the correct prepositions.

Describe Your Room

Describe the layout of your room using place prepositions.

Example: My bed is against the wall. There's a desk next to the window with a lamp on it. My bookshelf is between the closet and the door.

Giving Directions

Give directions from your home to a nearby location.

Example: To get to the supermarket, go straight along Main Street. Turn left at the traffic light. The supermarket is on your right, between the bank and the pharmacy.

Describe a Photo

Imagine a family photo and describe where everyone is positioned.

Example: In this photo, my father is standing in the center. My mother is next to him on the right. I'm sitting in front of them, and my dog is beside me.

Lost Item

Describe where you've looked for a lost item and where you finally found it.

Example: I looked for my keys in my bag and on the table. I checked under the sofa and behind the door. Finally, I found them inside the refrigerator!

Speaking Practice Tips

  • Be specific about locations and positions
  • Use a variety of prepositions, not just in, on, and at
  • Practice describing both indoor and outdoor locations
  • Try to create mental images as you speak
