Past Perfect Exercises

Question 1/10 Score: 0

Complete the sentence with the correct form of the past perfect:

Before the meeting started, the team ________ (prepare) all the necessary documents.

Correct!

Great job! The past perfect is used to describe an action that happened before another past action.

Not quite right.

The correct answer is "had prepared". The past perfect is used for actions completed before another past action.

Speaking Practice

Business Scenario:

You are in a job interview. Explain a challenging project you had completed before joining your previous company. Describe what you had learned from that experience.

Helpful Phrases:

  • Before joining the company, I had completed...
  • I had already learned how to...
  • By that time, I had developed skills in...
  • The project had taught me that...

Key Vocabulary:

implement collaborate deadline stakeholders overcome challenges achieve results
