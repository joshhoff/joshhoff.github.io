The Simple Past Tense

The simple past tense is used to talk about actions that were completed in the past.

Regular Verbs

For regular verbs, we add -ed to the base form:

  • walk → walked
  • talk → talked
  • play → played

Examples:

  • I walked to school yesterday.
  • She played tennis last weekend.

Irregular Verbs

Irregular verbs change in different ways:

Forming Questions

For questions, we use did + subject + base form:

  • Did you go to the party?
  • Did she eat lunch?

Forming Negatives

For negatives, we use did not (didn't) + base form:

  • I did not (didn't) go to the party.
  • She did not (didn't) eat lunch.

Die einfache Vergangenheitsform

Die einfache Vergangenheitsform wird verwendet, um über Handlungen zu sprechen, die in der Vergangenheit abgeschlossen wurden.

Regelmäßige Verben

Bei regelmäßigen Verben fügen wir -ed zur Grundform hinzu:

  • walk → walked
  • talk → talked
  • play → played

Beispiele:

  • Ich ging gestern zur Schule. (I walked to school yesterday.)
  • Sie spielte letztes Wochenende Tennis. (She played tennis last weekend.)

Unregelmäßige Verben

Unregelmäßige Verben ändern sich auf unterschiedliche Weise:

Grundform
Vergangenheit
Beispiel
go

Fragen bilden

Für Fragen verwenden wir did + Subjekt + Grundform:

  • Did you go to the party? (Bist du zur Party gegangen?)
  • Did she eat lunch? (Hat sie zu Mittag gegessen?)

Verneinungen bilden

Für Verneinungen verwenden wir did not (didn't) + Grundform:

  • I did not (didn't) go to the party. (Ich bin nicht zur Party gegangen.)
  • She did not (didn't) eat lunch. (Sie hat nicht zu Mittag gegessen.)
