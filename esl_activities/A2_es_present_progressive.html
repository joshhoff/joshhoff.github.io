What is the Present Progressive?

The Present Progressive (also called Present Continuous) tells us what is happening RIGHT NOW! 🕐


When do we use it?

  • Actions happening now: "I am eating lunch" 🍽️
  • Temporary situations: "She is staying with friends" 🏠
  • Future plans: "We are meeting tomorrow" 📅

¿Qué es el Presente Continuo?

El Presente Continuo nos dice lo que está pasando ¡AHORA MISMO! 🕐


¿Cuándo lo usamos?

  • Acciones que pasan ahora: "Estoy comiendo" 🍽️
  • Situaciones temporales: "Ella se está quedando con amigos" 🏠
  • Planes futuros: "Nos vamos a encontrar mañana" 📅
Quick Examples:
✅ "I am reading a book" (Yo estoy leyendo un libro)
✅ "They are playing soccer" (Ellos están jugando fútbol)
✅ "She is cooking dinner" (Ella está cocinando la cena)
Ejemplos Rápidos:
✅ "I am reading a book" (Yo estoy leyendo un libro)
✅ "They are playing soccer" (Ellos están jugando fútbol)

📚 How to Form the Present Progressive

📚 Cómo Formar el Presente Continuo

Formula: Subject + BE + Verb-ing


Step 1: Choose the correct form of "BE"

  • I → am
  • You/We/They → are
  • He/She/It → is

Step 2: Add "-ing" to the main verb

  • play → playing
  • eat → eating
  • run → running (double the consonant)
  • write → writing (drop the 'e')

Fórmula: Sujeto + BE + Verbo-ing


Paso 1: Elige la forma correcta de "BE"

  • I → am
  • You/We/They → are
  • He/She/It → is

Paso 2: Añade "-ing" al verbo principal

  • play → playing
  • eat → eating
  • run → running (dobla la consonante)
  • write → writing (quita la 'e')
Complete Examples:

📱 You are texting your friend
🎵 She is singing a song
⚽ They are playing football
📖 We are studying English
