Mirror Practice

To make the "ʃ" sound:

  1. Position your lips slightly forward, as if you're about to whistle.
  2. Keep your tongue raised near the roof of your mouth, but not touching it.
  3. Push air through the narrow space between your tongue and the roof of your mouth.
  4. The sound should be like a gentle "shhhh".
