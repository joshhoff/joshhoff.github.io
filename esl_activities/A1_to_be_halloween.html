🎃 Halloween "To Be" / Halloween "Ser/Estar" 🎃

Spooky English-Spanish Learning Adventure!

🧛‍♂️ The Spooky Verb "To Be" 🧛‍♀️

The most magical verb for Halloween! It tells us WHO the monsters are or HOW they feel!

👻 I am / Yo soy/estoy

Spooky Examples:

• I am a ghost 👻 / Yo soy un fantasma

• I am scared 😱 / Yo estoy asustado

• I am wearing a costume 🎭 / Llevo un disfraz

🧙‍♀️ You are / Tú eres/estás

Spooky Examples:

• You are a witch 🧙‍♀️ / Tú eres una bruja

• You are brave 💪 / Tú eres valiente

• You are trick-or-treating 🍬 / Estás pidiendo dulces

🧟‍♂️ He/She is / Él/Ella es/está

Spooky Examples:

• She is a vampire 🧛‍♀️ / Ella es una vampira

• He is hiding 🙈 / Él se está escondiendo

• She is carving pumpkins 🎃 / Ella está tallando calabazas

👥 We are / Nosotros somos/estamos

Spooky Examples:

• We are monsters 👹 / Somos monstruos

• We are excited 🎉 / Estamos emocionados

• We are going to a party 🎊 / Vamos a una fiesta

🕷️ They are / Ellos son/están

Spooky Examples:

• They are spiders 🕷️ / Son arañas

• They are creepy 😨 / Están espeluznantes

• They are collecting candy 🍭 / Están recolectando dulces

💬 Spooky Conversation Practice

🎭 Describe Your Halloween Costume

Practice saying these sentences about your costume:

  • I am a [ghost/witch/vampire/zombie]
  • I am wearing [black clothes/a cape/a mask]
  • I am scary/funny/creative
  • I am ready for Halloween!

👻 Describe Halloween Characters

Talk about spooky characters you see:

  • She is a beautiful witch 🧙‍♀️
  • He is a scary vampire 🧛‍♂️
  • They are friendly ghosts 👻
  • We are all monsters tonight! 👹

🎃 Express Halloween Feelings

Talk about how you feel during Halloween:

  • I am excited 🎉 / scared 😱 / happy 😊
  • You are brave 💪 / nervous 😰 / curious 🤔
  • She is surprised 😲 / frightened 😨
  • We are having fun 🎊 / being spooky 👻

🍭 Halloween Challenge Questions

Try to answer these spooky questions using "to be":

  • What is your favorite Halloween candy? 🍬
  • Where are you going trick-or-treating? 🏠
  • How are you feeling about Halloween? 🎃
  • Who is your favorite Halloween character? 🧛‍♂️
