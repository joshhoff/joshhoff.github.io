📚 How to Make Negative Sentences

🔑 The Magic Words: DON'T and DOESN'T

To say "NO" or "NOT", we add special helper words!

DON'T

I, You, We, They

DOESN'T

He, She, It

👥 Examples with I, You, We, They (DON'T)

⚽ "I play soccer." → ❌ "I don't play soccer."
📚 "You read books." → ❌ "You don't read books."
🏠 "We live here." → ❌ "We don't live here."
🎵 "They sing songs." → ❌ "They don't sing songs."

👤 Examples with He, She, It (DOESN'T)

🥛 "She likes milk." → ❌ "She doesn't like milk."
🚗 "He drives a car." → ❌ "He doesn't drive a car."
🌧️ "It rains today." → ❌ "It doesn't rain today."
🐕 "The dog barks." → ❌ "The dog doesn't bark."

🍎 More Fun Examples

🍕 "I eat pizza." → ❌ "I don't eat pizza."
📺 "She watches TV." → ❌ "She doesn't watch TV."
🏊 "They swim fast." → ❌ "They don't swim fast."
☕ "He drinks coffee." → ❌ "He doesn't drink coffee."
🎮 "We play games." → ❌ "We don't play games."
🌙 "It shines at night." → ❌ "It doesn't shine at night."

📝 Easy Pattern to Remember

Subject + DON'T/DOESN'T + Verb + Rest

Subject
I, You, He, She...
Helper
don't / doesn't
Verb
play, eat, like...
Rest
soccer, pizza...
