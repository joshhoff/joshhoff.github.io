Quick Reference Guide

💪 ABILITY

What someone can or cannot do

CAN: present ability
COULD: past ability

🤔 POSSIBILITY

What might happen or be true

MAY: formal possibility
MIGHT: weak possibility

📋 OBLIGATION

What someone must or should do

MUST: strong obligation
SHOULD: advice/recommendation

🤝 POLITENESS

Making requests politely

WOULD: very polite
COULD: polite requests
