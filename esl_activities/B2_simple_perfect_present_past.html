📖 Grammar Reference & Common Verbs

Simple Past

Use: Actions completed in the past at a specific time

Form: Subject + Past form of verb

✓ I walked to school yesterday.
✓ She finished her homework at 8 PM.

Present Perfect

Use: Actions that started in the past and continue to present, or past actions with present relevance

Form: Subject + have/has + Past participle

✓ I have lived here for five years.
✓ She has finished her project.

Past Perfect

Use: Actions completed before another past action

Form: Subject + had + Past participle

✓ I had eaten before she arrived.
✓ They had left by the time we got there.

💬 Conversation Practice

Practice using these tenses in conversation with these prompts:

🎯 Simple Past Practice:
Tell me about what you did last weekend. Use at least 5 different verbs in simple past tense.
⭐ Present Perfect Practice:
Describe your life experiences. What places have you visited? What skills have you learned? What achievements have you accomplished?
🔄 Past Perfect Practice:
Tell a story about arriving somewhere and finding that something had already happened. Example: "When I got to the party, most people had already left."
🎭 Mixed Tenses Challenge:
Describe a memorable day from your past. Include: what you had planned to do (past perfect), what actually happened (simple past), and how it has affected you since then (present perfect).
📱 Daily Routine Comparison:
Compare your routine now with your routine 5 years ago. What had you never done before that time? What have you started doing since then?
🌟 Achievement Story:
Tell about a goal you achieved. What had you done to prepare? What steps did you take? How has this achievement changed your life?
