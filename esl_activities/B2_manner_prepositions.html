📚 Prepositions of Manner

Master advanced prepositions that describe how actions are performed

Prepositions of Manner - Contextual Definitions

BY + gerund/noun
Usage: Describes the method or means of doing something
• She improved her English by watching movies with subtitles.
• The problem was solved by teamwork and persistence.
• He travels to work by bicycle every day.
• The company increased profits by implementing innovative strategies.
• She calmed her nerves by taking deep breaths before the interview.
• The chef enhanced the flavor by adding fresh herbs and spices.
• Students can master grammar by practicing regularly and seeking feedback.
• The negotiation succeeded by finding common ground between parties.
WITH + noun
Usage: Indicates the tool, instrument, or manner used to perform an action
• She painted the masterpiece with incredible precision.
• He spoke with confidence during the presentation.
• The surgeon operated with steady hands.
• The teacher explained the concept with patience and clarity.
• She handled the crisis with remarkable composure and wisdom.
• The pianist performed with extraordinary skill and emotion.
• He approached the challenge with determination and creativity.
• The diplomat negotiated with tact and cultural sensitivity.
• She writes with eloquence and sophisticated vocabulary.
IN + manner/style
Usage: Describes the style, manner, or way something is done
• The dancers moved in perfect harmony.
• She always dresses in an elegant style.
• The team worked in complete silence.
• He delivered the speech in a calm and measured tone.
• The orchestra played in perfect synchronization.
• She responded to criticism in a professional manner.
• The children behaved in an exemplary fashion during the ceremony.
• The artist works in a very unconventional and experimental style.
• He communicates in a direct and straightforward way.
THROUGH + method/process
Usage: Indicates the process or method by which something is achieved
• Success comes through hard work and dedication.
• She learned the language through immersion.
• The message was conveyed through body language.
• Understanding develops through careful observation and analysis.
• The company achieved growth through strategic partnerships.
• Healing occurs through time, patience, and proper treatment.
• She expressed her emotions through poetry and creative writing.
• Social change happens through collective action and persistence.
• Knowledge is transmitted through education and mentorship.
WITHOUT + noun/gerund
Usage: Describes the absence of a method, tool, or manner
• She solved the complex equation without using a calculator.
• He delivered the presentation without any notes or preparation.
• The artist created the sculpture without traditional tools.
• She navigated the city without relying on GPS technology.
• The team completed the project without external assistance.
• He communicated his ideas without speaking a single word.
VIA + method/route
Usage: Indicates the means, channel, or route through which something is done
• The information was transmitted via satellite communication.
• She learned about the opportunity via social media networks.
• The payment was processed via secure online banking.
• Students can submit assignments via the digital platform.
• The message reached millions via television broadcast.
• Feedback is collected via anonymous surveys and interviews.
ACCORDING TO + source/method
Usage: Indicates the source, standard, or method used as a basis for action
• The project was executed according to industry best practices.
• She organized the event according to traditional customs.
• The research was conducted according to ethical guidelines.
• He prepared the dish according to his grandmother's recipe.
• The building was constructed according to sustainable principles.
• Decisions are made according to democratic processes.
