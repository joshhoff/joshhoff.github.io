Master advanced prepositions that describe how actions are performed

Prepositions of Manner - Contextual Definitions

BY + gerund/noun
Usage: Describes the method or means of doing something
• She improved her English by watching movies with subtitles.
• The problem was solved by teamwork and persistence.
• He travels to work by bicycle every day.
WITH + noun
Usage: Indicates the tool, instrument, or manner used to perform an action
• She painted the masterpiece with incredible precision.
• He spoke with confidence during the presentation.
• The surgeon operated with steady hands.
IN + manner/style
Usage: Describes the style, manner, or way something is done
• The dancers moved in perfect harmony.
• She always dresses in an elegant style.
• The team worked in complete silence.
THROUGH + method/process
Usage: Indicates the process or method by which something is achieved
• Success comes through hard work and dedication.
• She learned the language through immersion.
• The message was conveyed through body language.
