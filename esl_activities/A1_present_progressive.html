🏙️ Present Progressive Town Explorer

📚 What is Present Progressive?

The Formula

Subject + am/is/are + verb + -ing

Use it for: Actions happening RIGHT NOW or around this time

Time words: now, right now, at the moment, today, this week

Examples Around Town

🏥 The doctor is helping a patient at the hospital.
👮 Police officers are working at the police station.
🏦 I am waiting in line at the bank.
📚 Students are studying at the library right now.
🏪 She is shopping at the supermarket.
📮 He is sending a package at the post office.
🍽️ They are eating lunch at the restaurant.
⛽ We are getting gas at the gas station.

✍️ Practice Exercises

🏥 1. The nurse _______ (check) the patient's temperature at the hospital.
📚 2. I _______ (read) a book at the library right now.
🏦 3. My parents _______ (open) a new account at the bank.
🍽️ 4. The waiter _______ (bring) our food at the restaurant.
👮 5. Police officers _______ (patrol) the streets this week.
📮 6. She _______ (mail) a letter at the post office.

💬 Conversation Practice

🏥 At the Hospital

Receptionist: Good morning! How can I help you?
Patient: I'm waiting for Dr. Smith. Is she working today?
Receptionist: Yes, she's examining a patient right now. She'll be ready in 10 minutes.

💡Your turn! Practice these questions:

• What is the doctor doing?

• Who are you waiting for?

• Is the nurse helping someone now?

🏦 At the Bank

Teller: Next, please! Are you opening a new account?
Customer: No, I'm depositing money into my savings account.
Teller: Great! I'm processing your deposit now.

💡Your turn! Practice these questions:

• What are you doing at the bank?

• Is the teller helping you?

• Are they opening or closing now?

📚 At the Library

Librarian: Excuse me, the students over there are making noise. They're studying for exams.
Visitor: Sorry! I'm looking for books about history. Can you help me?
Librarian: Of course! I'm organizing the history section right now. Follow me.

💡Your turn! Practice these questions:

• What are the students doing?

• What is the librarian organizing?

• Are you reading or studying?

🍽️ At the Restaurant

Server: Good evening! Are you celebrating something special tonight?
Customer: Yes! We're celebrating my mom's birthday. She's turning 50!
Server: Wonderful! The chef is preparing a special dessert for her right now.

💡Your turn! Practice these questions:

• What are you celebrating?

• Is the chef cooking something special?

• What are the people at that table eating?
