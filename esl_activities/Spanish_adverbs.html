🎯 Spanish Adverb Rules

1. Formation with -mente

Add -mente to the feminine form of adjectives:

rápido → rápida → rápidamente (quickly)

fácil → fácilmente (easily)

2. Position in Sentences

Adverbs usually come after the verb they modify:

Ella habla claramente. (She speaks clearly.)

Corremos rápidamente. (We run quickly.)

3. Common Irregular Adverbs

Some adverbs don't follow the -mente pattern:

bien (well)

mal (badly)

mucho (a lot)

poco (little)

muy (very)

bastante (quite)

4. Time and Frequency Adverbs

siempre (always)

nunca (never)

a veces (sometimes)

ya (already)

todavía (still)

pronto (soon)
