👤 Subject Pronouns

Introduction

Subject pronouns replace the subject of a sentence. They perform the action of the verb and answer "who" or "what" is doing the action.

Explanation

Singular:

  • I - first person
  • You - second person
  • He/She/It - third person

Plural:

  • We - first person plural
  • You - second person plural
  • They - third person plural

Practice

Fill in the blank with the correct subject pronoun:

___ is a doctor. (Maria)

Conversation Prompts

  • • Describe your family using subject pronouns: "She is my sister, he is my brother..."
  • • Talk about your friends' hobbies: "They love playing soccer, she enjoys reading..."
  • • Discuss daily routines: "I wake up early, we have breakfast together..."

🎯 Object Pronouns

Introduction

Object pronouns receive the action of the verb. They replace the object in a sentence and answer "whom" or "what" receives the action.

Explanation

Singular:

  • Me - receives action (I → me)
  • You - receives action
  • Him/Her/It - receives action

Plural:

  • Us - receives action (we → us)
  • You - receives action
  • Them - receives action (they → them)

Practice

Fill in the blank with the correct subject pronoun:

The teacher called ___. (the students)

Conversation Prompts

  • • Describe who helps you: "My parents support me, my teacher guides us..."
  • • Talk about giving gifts: "I bought her a book, they gave him a watch..."
  • • Discuss invitations: "She invited them to dinner, we called you yesterday..."

🪞 Reflexive Pronouns

Introduction

Reflexive pronouns are used when the subject and object of a sentence are the same person or thing. They end in -self (singular) or -selves (plural).

Explanation

Singular:

  • Myself - I do to myself
  • Yourself - you do to yourself
  • Himself/Herself/Itself - he/she/it does to self

Plural:

  • Ourselves - we do to ourselves
  • Yourselves - you (plural) do to yourselves
  • Themselves - they do to themselves

Practice

Fill in the blank with the correct subject pronoun:

She looked at ___ in the mirror.

Conversation Prompts

  • • Describe your morning routine: "I wash myself, I dress myself..."
  • • Talk about learning: "We taught ourselves to cook, they prepared themselves for the exam..."
  • • Discuss self-care: "She takes care of herself, you should believe in yourself..."

❓ Indefinite Pronouns

Introduction

Indefinite pronouns refer to non-specific people, places, or things. They don't refer to a particular person or thing.

Explanation

People:

  • Someone/Somebody
  • Anyone/Anybody
  • Everyone/Everybody
  • No one/Nobody

Things:

  • Something
  • Anything
  • Everything
  • Nothing

Places:

  • Somewhere
  • Anywhere
  • Everywhere
  • Nowhere

Practice

Fill in the blank with the correct subject pronoun:

___ is knocking at the door.

Conversation Prompts

  • • Describe mysterious situations: "Someone called me, but nobody answered..."
  • • Talk about searching: "I looked everywhere for my keys, but found nothing..."
  • • Discuss general statements: "Everyone loves pizza, anybody can learn this..."

🏠 Possessive Pronouns

Introduction

Possessive pronouns show ownership or possession. They replace possessive adjectives + nouns and stand alone in sentences.

Explanation

Singular:

  • Mine - belongs to me
  • Yours - belongs to you
  • His/Hers/Its - belongs to him/her/it

Plural:

  • Ours - belongs to us
  • Yours - belongs to you (plural)
  • Theirs - belongs to them

Practice

Fill in the blank with the correct subject pronoun:

This book is my book. → This book is ___.

Conversation Prompts

  • • Compare belongings: "This car is mine, that house is theirs..."
  • • Discuss ownership: "The responsibility is ours, the decision is yours..."
  • • Talk about preferences: "My favorite is this one, what's yours?"
