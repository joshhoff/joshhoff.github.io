📚Warm-Up: Quick Review

Articles (a, an, the, Ø)

a/an: Use before singular countable nouns when mentioning for the first time (a book, an apple).

the: Use when referring to something specific or already mentioned (the book we discussed).

Ø (no article): Use with plural nouns or uncountable nouns in general (love, happiness, books).

Common Prepositions

in/on/at: in (months, years, cities), on (days, dates), at (specific times, places)

to/for: to (direction, recipient), for (purpose, duration)

of/about: of (possession, part of), about (topic, concerning)
