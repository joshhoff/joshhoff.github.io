Present Simple, Present Continuous & Have Got

📝 Present Simple

Use: For habits, routines, facts, and things that are always true.

Form: Subject + verb (add -s/-es for he/she/it)

💡 Para hispanohablantes: Similar al presente simple en español, pero recuerda añadir -s/-es con he/she/it (él/ella).

Examples:

✓ I work every day. (Yo trabajo todos los días.)
✓ She studies English. (Ella estudia inglés.)
✓ They live in Madrid. (Ellos viven en Madrid.)
✓ The sun rises in the east. (El sol sale por el este.)

📋 Activity 1: Choose the correct form

1. Maria _____ to school every morning.
2. We _____ Spanish at home.

💬 Conversation Practice

Ask your partner: What do you do every day? (¿Qué haces todos los días?)
Talk about: Your daily routine - "I wake up at..., I eat breakfast..., I go to..."
Discuss: What does your family do on weekends?

⏰ Present Continuous

Use: For actions happening RIGHT NOW or temporary situations.

Form: Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

💡 Para hispanohablantes: Similar a "estar + gerundio" en español (estoy comiendo, está trabajando).

Examples:

✓ I am studying now. (Estoy estudiando ahora.)
✓ She is reading a book. (Ella está leyendo un libro.)
✓ They are playing soccer. (Ellos están jugando fútbol.)
✓ We are learning English this year. (Estamos aprendiendo inglés este año.)

📋 Activity 2: Fill in the blanks

1. Right now, I (study) English.
2. What are you doing right now?

💬 Conversation Practice

Ask your partner: What are you doing right now? (¿Qué estás haciendo ahora?)
Describe: What is happening in the classroom right now?
Talk about: What are you learning this month?

🎒 Have Got

Use: To talk about possession (things you own) and relationships.

Form: Subject + have/has + got

Note: "Have got" = "Have" (same meaning, British English prefers "have got")

💡 Para hispanohablantes: Equivale a "tener" en español. "I have got a car" = "Tengo un coche."

Examples:

✓ I have got a dog. (Tengo un perro.)
✓ She has got two brothers. (Ella tiene dos hermanos.)
✓ We have got a new teacher. (Tenemos un profesor nuevo.)
✓ They have got blue eyes. (Ellos tienen ojos azules.)

Questions & Negatives:

❓ Have you got a pen? (¿Tienes un bolígrafo?)
❓ Has she got a car? (¿Tiene ella un coche?)
✗ I haven't got a bike. (No tengo una bicicleta.)
✗ He hasn't got time. (Él no tiene tiempo.)

💬 Conversation Practice

Ask your partner: Have you got any brothers or sisters? (¿Tienes hermanos o hermanas?)
Talk about: What have you got in your bag?
Describe: Your family - "I have got..., My mother has got..., My father has got..."

🔄 Quick Comparison

Present Simple: Habits & facts → "I work every day"

Present Continuous: Now & temporary → "I am working now"

Have Got: Possession → "I have got a job"
