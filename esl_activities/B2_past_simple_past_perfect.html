Grammar Rules & Usage

Present Perfect

Form: have/has + past participle

Use for:

  • Life experiences (no specific time)
  • Actions with present relevance
  • Unfinished time periods

Examples:

"I have run three marathons."

"She has trained for six months."

Past Simple

Form: verb + -ed (or irregular past form)

Use for:

  • Completed actions at specific times
  • Past habits or routines
  • Sequence of past events

Examples:

"I ran a marathon last year."

"He trained every morning in 2022."

🗣️ Speaking Prompts

Present Perfect Questions

Use these for life experiences (no specific time mentioned)

Have you ever run a marathon?

Have you ever tried rock climbing?

Have you ever joined a gym?

Have you ever injured yourself while exercising?

Have you ever participated in a fitness challenge?

Past Simple Questions

Use these for specific memories and times

When did you start your fitness journey?

What sport did you play in school?

How did you feel after your first long run?

Where did you exercise last week?

What motivated you to start training?

📖 Task 1: Write Your Training History

Instructions:

  • Write a paragraph (150-200 words) about your fitness and training experiences
  • Use both present perfect and past simple tenses
  • Include specific examples and time references where appropriate
  • Focus on marathon training, gym experiences, or any fitness activities

Helpful Prompts:

Present Perfect Ideas:

  • • Life experiences with fitness
  • • Recent achievements
  • • Changes you've noticed

Past Simple Ideas:

  • • When you started training
  • • Specific events or races
  • • Your routine last year

💭 Task 2: Compare & Contrast

Instructions:

  • Compare your fitness habits from the past with your current habits (100-150 words)
  • Use past simple for old habits and present perfect for recent changes
  • Include time expressions like "used to," "now," "recently," "in the past"
  • Focus on specific changes in your routine, diet, or exercise preferences

Helpful Sentence Starters:

Past Habits:

  • • "I used to..."
  • • "In the past, I..."
  • • "When I was younger, I..."

Current Changes:

  • • "I have started..."
  • • "Recently, I have..."
  • • "I have become..."

🎯 Task 3: Goal Setting

Instructions:

  • Write about your fitness goals and what you have achieved so far (120-180 words)
  • Use present perfect for achievements and progress made
  • Use past simple for specific moments when you set goals or completed challenges
  • Include both short-term and long-term fitness objectives

Writing Prompts:

💪 Achievement Focus: What fitness milestones have you reached? When did you first set these goals?

🏃‍♂️ Progress Focus: How has your fitness improved over time? What specific improvements have you noticed?

🎯 Future Focus: What are your current goals? How have your priorities changed since you started?
