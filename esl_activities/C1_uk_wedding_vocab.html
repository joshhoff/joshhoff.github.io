Conversation Practice

🎭 Role Play: Wedding Planner

You are a wedding planner meeting with a couple. Use vocabulary words like "aisle," "tier," "cluster," and "reception" to discuss their wedding plans. Practice explaining different ceremony layouts and decoration options.

💬 Discussion Questions

1. Describe your ideal wedding ceremony. Where would guests sit? How would the aisle be decorated?

2. What makes a wedding dress "high key" attention-grabbing? Discuss different styles and their impact.

3. How would you arrange flowers in clusters around the venue? What colors would be most magnetic to guests?

🎯 Vocabulary Challenge

Create a 2-minute speech about planning the perfect wedding reception. You must use at least 8 words from your vocabulary list. Focus on describing the venue, decorations, and atmosphere.

📝 Storytelling Prompt

Tell the story of a memorable wedding you attended (real or imaginary). Include details about walking down the aisle, the ceremony setup, the reception venue, and what made it special. Use descriptive vocabulary to paint a vivid picture.

🤔 Compare and Contrast

Compare traditional weddings in your culture with modern destination weddings. Discuss differences in ceremony structure, venue choices, and decoration styles. Use vocabulary words to describe specific elements.

🎪 Debate Topic

"Large, elaborate weddings are better than small, intimate ceremonies." Take a side and argue your position using wedding vocabulary. Consider factors like venue size, guest arrangements, and overall atmosphere.
