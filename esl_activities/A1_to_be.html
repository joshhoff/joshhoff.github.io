🎭 The Verb "To Be" 🎭

The most important verb in English! It tells us WHO someone is or HOW they are.

👤 I am / Yo soy/estoy

Examples:

• I am happy 😊 / Yo estoy feliz

• I am a student 📚 / Yo soy estudiante

👥 You are / Tú eres/estás

Examples:

• You are kind 💝 / Tú eres amable

• You are tired 😴 / Tú estás cansado

👨👩 He/She is / Él/Ella es/está

Examples:

• She is tall 📏 / Ella es alta

• He is hungry 🍕 / Él está hambriento

👫 We are / Nosotros somos/estamos

Examples:

• We are friends 👫 / Somos amigos

• We are ready 🚀 / Estamos listos

👥 They are / Ellos son/están

Examples:

• They are teachers 👩‍🏫 / Son maestros

• They are excited 🎉 / Están emocionados

💬 Conversation Practice

🗣️ Introduce Yourself

Practice saying these sentences about yourself:

  • I am [your name]
  • I am [your age] years old
  • I am from [your country]
  • I am a [student/teacher/etc.]

👥 Describe Others

Look around and describe people:

  • She is tall / He is short
  • They are friends
  • You are very kind
  • We are classmates

😊 Express Feelings

Talk about how you or others feel:

  • I am happy 😊 / sad 😢 / excited 🎉
  • You are tired 😴 / hungry 🍕 / thirsty 🥤
  • She is surprised 😲 / confused 🤔
  • We are ready 🚀 / busy 💼

🎯 Challenge Questions

Try to answer these questions using "to be":

  • What is your favorite color?
  • Where are you from?
  • How are you today?
  • Who is your best friend?
