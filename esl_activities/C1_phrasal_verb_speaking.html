Phrasal Verb Speaking Practice

Practice your English speaking skills with phrasal verbs

Instructions

Speak for 1–2 minutes about a real situation in your life. Use at least 5 of the phrasal verbs while you speak. Try to speak naturally and clearly.

Tips for Speaking:

  • Try to include emotions (Were you frustrated? Relieved? Embarrassed?)
  • Use past tenses where needed
  • If you forget a word, just describe it the best you can—fluency matters more than perfection!

Choose a Speaking Prompt

Misunderstanding or Mistake

Personal

"Tell me about a time when you had a misunderstanding with someone, made a mistake, or had to fix a difficult situation. What happened? How did you deal with it? What did you learn from it?"

Career Challenge

Work

"Describe a challenging project or task at work/school. How did you prepare for it? What obstacles did you face? How did you overcome them?"

Learning Something New

Education

"Talk about a time when you learned a new skill or hobby. Why did you start? What difficulties did you encounter? How did you improve over time?"

Travel Experience

Adventure

"Describe a memorable trip or travel experience. Where did you go? What did you do? Did anything unexpected happen? What made it special?"

Future Plans

Goals

"Talk about your plans for the next few years. What are you working towards? What steps are you taking to achieve your goals? What challenges do you expect?"

Solving a Problem

Challenge

"Describe a problem you had to solve recently. What was the issue? How did you approach it? What was the outcome?"

Self-Evaluation

After completing your speaking practice, ask yourself:

  • Did I use at least 5 phrasal verbs correctly?
  • Was my speech clear and natural?
  • Did I stay on topic and answer all parts of the prompt?
  • What could I improve for next time?

Remember: Regular practice is key to improving your speaking skills. Try to practice with different phrasal verbs every day!
