What are Subject Pronouns?

Subject pronouns are words that replace the subject noun in a sentence. They tell us who is performing the action of the verb.

I (yo)

Used when talking about yourself

He / She (él / ella)

Used when talking about another person

We (nosotros)

Used when talking about yourself and others

They (ellos/ellas)

Used when talking about multiple other people

You (tú)

Used when talking to someone

Examples with Subject Pronouns

Here are some examples of how to use subject pronouns with our verbs:

I (yo)

  • • I run in the park. (Yo corro en el parque.)
  • • I eat breakfast every morning. (Yo como el desayuno cada mañana.)
  • • I play soccer with my friends. (Yo juego al fútbol con mis amigos.)

He / She (él / ella)

  • • He walks to school. (Él camina a la escuela.)
  • • She jumps rope. (Ella salta la cuerda.)
  • • He sleeps eight hours. (Él duerme ocho horas.)

We (nosotros)

  • • We walk in the forest. (Nosotros caminamos en el bosque.)
  • • We eat dinner together. (Nosotros comemos la cena juntos.)
  • • We play games on weekends. (Nosotros jugamos a juegos los fines de semana.)

They (ellos/ellas)

  • • They run every morning. (Ellos corren cada mañana.)
  • • They sleep in the hotel. (Ellos duermen en el hotel.)
  • • They jump into the pool. (Ellos saltan a la piscina.)

You (tú)

  • • You walk quickly. (Tú caminas rápidamente.)
  • • You eat healthy food. (Tú comes comida saludable.)
  • • You play the guitar. (Tú juegas la guitarra.)

Conversation Practice

Practice this conversation with a partner or read both parts aloud:

Person A: Hello! What do you do in the morning?

Person B: I eat breakfast and then I run in the park.

Person A: That's nice. Does your friend run too?

Person B: No, she walks in the park. She doesn't like to run.

Person A: What about your parents?

Person B: They sleep late. They don't exercise in the morning.

Person A: My brother and I play soccer in the afternoon.

Person B: We play soccer too! Maybe we can play together.
