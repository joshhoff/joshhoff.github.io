What are Vowel Sounds?

Vowels are sounds you make with your mouth open. In English, the vowel letters are A, E, I, O, U. But there are more vowel sounds than letters! Your mouth shape changes for each sound.

What are Consonant Sounds?

Consonants are sounds you make by blocking air with your lips, teeth, or tongue. Some consonants are voiced (your throat vibrates) and some are voiceless (no vibration). Touch your throat to feel the difference!

What are Diphthongs?

Diphthongs are two vowel sounds together in one syllable. Your mouth starts in one position and glides to another. Think of them as "moving vowels." Listen carefully — they sound like one smooth sound!
