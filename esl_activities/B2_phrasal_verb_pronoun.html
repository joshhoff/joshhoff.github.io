📖 Quick Grammar Rules

Separable Phrasal Verbs

Pronoun goes BETWEEN the verb and particle:

• "Turn it off" ✓

• "Turn off it" ✗

Inseparable Phrasal Verbs

Pronoun goes AFTER the particle:

• "Look after it" ✓

• "Look it after" ✗
