Present Tense Grammar Review

How to Form Present Tense

Positive Sentences

I/You/We/They: verb (base form)
• I sit on the chair
• They clean the table
He/She/It: verb + s
• She sits on the sofa
• He cleans the window

Negative Sentences

I/You/We/They: don't + verb
• I don't sleep on the sofa
• They don't eat in the bedroom
He/She/It: doesn't + verb
• She doesn't sit on the table
• He doesn't open the window

Question Forms

Do/Does + subject + verb?
• Do you sit on the chair?
• Does she sleep in the bed?
Where/What/When + do/does + subject + verb?
• Where do you eat?
• What does he clean?

Conversation Practice

🗣️ Speaking Prompts

Practice these questions with a partner or record yourself:

Beginner Level:
  • • What furniture do you have in your bedroom?
  • • Where do you eat breakfast?
  • • Do you have a comfortable chair?
Intermediate Level:
  • • Describe your living room furniture.
  • • What do you do when you sit on your sofa?
  • • How often do you clean your furniture?

💭 Role Play Scenarios

Scenario 1: Shopping for Furniture

Person A: You work in a furniture store. Person B: You want to buy a new sofa.

Useful phrases:
• "What kind of sofa do you need?"
• "I want a comfortable chair for my living room."
• "How much does this table cost?"
Scenario 2: Describing Your Home

Take turns describing the furniture in different rooms of your home.

Useful phrases:
• "In my bedroom, I have a bed and a chair."
• "My kitchen table is small but comfortable."
• "I don't have a sofa, but I have two chairs."
