🪞 What are Reflexive Verbs?

Reflexive verbs (verbos reflexivos) indicate that the subject performs an action on itself. They use reflexive pronouns: me, te, se, nos, os, se.

Example: "Me levanto" = I get (myself) up

The action reflects back to the subject
