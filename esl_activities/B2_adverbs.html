Discussion Prompt

  • Use frequency adverbs: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never
  • Use manner adverbs: quickly, slowly, carefully, loudly, quietly
  • Place frequency adverbs before the main verb: "I usually wake up early."
  • Place manner adverbs after the verb or object: "She read the letter carefully."
