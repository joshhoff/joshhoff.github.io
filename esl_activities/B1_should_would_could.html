📚 Quick Grammar Guide

SHOULD

Advice, recommendations, or what is right to do

"You should rest more."

COULD

Possibility, ability, or polite suggestions

"We could go tomorrow."

WOULD

Hypotheticals, preferences, or polite requests

"I would love to help."
