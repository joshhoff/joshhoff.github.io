🔄 Frequency Adverbs Adverbios de Frecuencia

always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never

• Before main verbs: • Antes del verbo principal:

"I always drink coffee in the morning."

• After 'be' verbs: • Después del verbo 'be':

"She is usually on time."

🎭 Manner Adverbs Adverbios de Modo

quickly, slowly, carefully, loudly, quietly

• Usually at the end of sentences: • Generalmente al final de las oraciones:

"He speaks English fluently."

"She completed the task carefully."

⏰ Time Adverbs Adverbios de Tiempo

yesterday, today, tomorrow, now, later, recently

• At the beginning or end: • Al principio o al final:

"Yesterday I went shopping."

"I will call you later."

💡 Quick Tip 💡 Consejo Rápido

When in doubt, frequency adverbs usually go before the main verb, and manner adverbs go at the end of the sentence! ¡Cuando tengas dudas, los adverbios de frecuencia generalmente van antes del verbo principal, y los adverbios de modo van al final de la oración!
