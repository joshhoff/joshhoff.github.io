English Prefixes

What are Prefixes?

Prefixes are added to the beginning of a word to modify its meaning. Both English and Russian use prefixes, but they don't always correspond directly.

English-Russian Comparison:

In Russian, prefixes (приставки) are extremely important for verb aspect and motion verbs. In English, prefixes more commonly modify meaning without changing the grammatical function.

English Suffixes

What are Suffixes?

Suffixes are added to the end of a word to change its meaning or part of speech. Both English and Russian use suffixes extensively.

English-Russian Comparison:

In Russian, suffixes (суффиксы) often indicate grammatical gender, diminutives, and emotional connotations. English suffixes typically change the word class or add specific meanings without the gender distinctions found in Russian.

-er/-or

Meaning: person who does something

Russian equivalent: -тель, -щик

Examples: teacherучитель, actorактёр

-able/-ible

Meaning: capable of being

Russian equivalent: -емый, -имый

Examples: readableчитаемый, flexibleгибкий

-ment

Meaning: state or result

Russian equivalent: -ние, -ство

Examples: developmentразвитие, agreementсоглашение

-tion/-sion

Meaning: state or quality

Russian equivalent: -ция, -сия

Examples: educationобразование, decisionрешение

-ful

Key Differences Between English and Russian Affixes

Feature English Russian
Gender Marking No grammatical gender in suffixes Suffixes often indicate gender (-ый, -ая, -ое)
Verb Aspect Not marked by prefixes Prefixes often change aspect (по-, с-, на-)
Diminutives Limited (-y, -ie: doggy) Extensive system (-чик, -ок, -ушка)
Emotional Connotation Rarely conveyed through affixes Often conveyed (-ище for augmentative)
Spelling Changes Sometimes occur (happy → happiness) More frequent consonant mutations

Speaking Practice Activities

Word Building Chain

In pairs, take turns adding prefixes or suffixes to create new words from a base word.

Example:

Student 1: "care"

Student 2: "careful"

Student 1: "carefully"

Student 2: "carelessness"

Prefix-Suffix Story

Create a short story using as many words with prefixes and suffixes as possible.

Prompt:

Tell a story about an unsuccessful job interview that had an unexpected ending.

Translation Challenge

Translate sentences with affixed words between English and Russian, discussing the differences.

Example:

"The unreliable information misled the researchers."

"Ненадежная информация ввела исследователей в заблуждение."

Discuss how the prefixes "un-" and "mis-" are translated into Russian.

Debate with Affixes

Debate a topic using words with specific prefixes or suffixes in each sentence.

Topic:

"Is social media helpful or harmful?"

Use words with -ful, -less, re-, and un- in your arguments.

Speaking Prompts

Personal Experience

  • Describe a misunderstanding you had with someone and how you resolved it.
  • Talk about an unforgettable experience that transformed your outlook on life.
  • Discuss the most valuable relationship in your life and why it's meaningful.

Opinion & Discussion

  • Is it better to be overqualified or underqualified for a job? Why?
  • Should international travel be mandatory in educational programs?
  • How has technology transformed communication in modern society?
