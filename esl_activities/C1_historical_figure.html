Regret & Reality

Advanced ESL Role Play for C1 Learners

How to Use This Tool

1. Generate a Character

Click "Generate Character" to receive a historical figure with a backstory and current dilemma.

2. Express Regrets

Use the "Generate Regret Prompt" button to get example regrets using third conditional structures.

Write your own regrets from the character's perspective using structures like:

"If I had..., I would have..."

"Had I known..., I might have..."

3. Explain Reality

Switch to "Reality Check" mode to describe the actual consequences of the character's actions.

Use narrative tenses (past simple, past continuous, past perfect) to explain what really happened.

Grammar Focus:

  • Third conditional (If + past perfect, would/could/might + have + past participle)
  • Mixed conditionals
  • Past perfect for sequence of events
  • Narrative tenses

Role Play Activity

Example Regrets:

Click "Generate Regret Prompt" to see example regrets for your character.

Write Your Regrets:

Express 2-3 regrets from your character's perspective using third conditional structures.

Suggested structures:

  • "If I had..., I would have..."
  • "Had I known..., I might have..."
  • "I wish I had/hadn't..."

Reality Prompt:

Click "Generate Reality Prompt" to see a prompt about the actual consequences.

Describe Reality:

Explain what actually happened as a result of your character's actions using narrative tenses.

Remember to use:

  • Past simple for completed actions
  • Past continuous for ongoing actions
  • Past perfect for actions before another past action
