General Guidelines (But Not Rules)

Helpful patterns to guess if a phrasal verb is separable or not.

Phrasal verbs can be separable or inseparable. This affects where you place pronouns.

These guidelines will help you make good guesses, but remember - they are not strict rules!

Guideline 1

Separable verbs often involve physical actions

turn off

Turn off the light.

Turn it off. ✓

Turn off it. ✗

pick up

Pick up the book.

Pick it up. ✓

Pick up it. ✗

Guideline 2

Inseparable verbs often involve emotions, abstract ideas, or relationships

look after

Look after your mom.

Look after her. ✓

Look her after. ✗

get over

Get over your fear.

Get over it. ✓

Get it over. ✗

Guideline 3

Try the pronoun trick

When in doubt, try replacing the noun with a pronoun:

Turn off the light.

Replace "the light" with "it"

Which sounds correct?

If the pronoun sounds better between the verb and particle, the phrasal verb is likely separable.

If the pronoun sounds better after the particle, the phrasal verb is likely inseparable.
