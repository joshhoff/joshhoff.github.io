Professional English for Drone Flying Consultants

Common Business Sentence Starters

Introducing Services

English: "We specialize in..." / "Our company provides..." / "I would like to offer..."
Español: "Nos especializamos en..." / "Nuestra empresa proporciona..." / "Me gustaría ofrecer..."
Example: "We specialize in aerial photography for real estate projects."
Ejemplo: "Nos especializamos en fotografía aérea para proyectos inmobiliarios."

Explaining Procedures

English: "First, we need to..." / "The process involves..." / "Before we begin..."
Español: "Primero, necesitamos..." / "El proceso involucra..." / "Antes de comenzar..."
Example: "Before we begin the flight, we need to check weather conditions."
Ejemplo: "Antes de comenzar el vuelo, necesitamos verificar las condiciones climáticas."

Safety and Regulations

English: "For safety reasons..." / "According to regulations..." / "It's important to note that..."
Español: "Por razones de seguridad..." / "Según las regulaciones..." / "Es importante notar que..."
Example: "For safety reasons, we cannot fly in restricted airspace."
Ejemplo: "Por razones de seguridad, no podemos volar en espacio aéreo restringido."

Discussing Pricing

English: "Our rates start at..." / "The cost depends on..." / "I can offer you a package deal..."
Español: "Nuestras tarifas comienzan en..." / "El costo depende de..." / "Puedo ofrecerle un paquete..."
Example: "Our rates start at $200 per hour, but the cost depends on the project complexity."
Ejemplo: "Nuestras tarifas comienzan en $200 por hora, pero el costo depende de la complejidad del proyecto."

Project Timeline

English: "The project will take..." / "We can deliver by..." / "The timeline includes..."
Español: "El proyecto tomará..." / "Podemos entregar para..." / "El cronograma incluye..."
Example: "The project will take 2-3 days, and we can deliver the final images by Friday."
Ejemplo: "El proyecto tomará 2-3 días, y podemos entregar las imágenes finales para el viernes."

Technical Explanations

English: "The equipment we use..." / "This technology allows us to..." / "The resolution will be..."
Español: "El equipo que usamos..." / "Esta tecnología nos permite..." / "La resolución será..."
Example: "The equipment we use captures 4K video and 20-megapixel still images."
Ejemplo: "El equipo que usamos captura video 4K e imágenes fijas de 20 megapíxeles."

Follow-up and Maintenance

English: "After completion..." / "We also provide..." / "For future projects..."
Español: "Después de completar..." / "También proporcionamos..." / "Para proyectos futuros..."
Example: "After completion, we provide all raw footage and edited highlights within 48 hours."
Ejemplo: "Después de completar, proporcionamos todo el material sin editar y los aspectos destacados editados en 48 horas."

Written Practice Activities

Activity 1: Service Description

Write a short paragraph describing your drone services to a potential client. Use at least 3 sentence starters from above.

Activity 2: Safety Explanation

Explain to a client why you cannot fly in certain weather conditions. Use professional language.

Activity 3: Project Proposal

Write a brief proposal for an aerial photography project. Include timeline, equipment, and deliverables.

Conversation Practice Scenarios

Scenario 1: Initial Client Consultation

Situation: A real estate agent calls to inquire about aerial photography services for a luxury property listing.

Practice: Role-play both sides of this conversation. Focus on:

  • Introducing your services professionally
  • Asking about the client's specific needs
  • Explaining your process and timeline
  • Discussing pricing and availability

Scenario 2: Weather Delay Explanation

Situation: You need to postpone a scheduled drone flight due to high winds and explain this to your client.

Practice: Practice explaining technical safety requirements in simple terms.

  • Explaining weather safety requirements
  • Apologizing professionally for the delay
  • Offering alternative dates
  • Reassuring the client about quality

Scenario 3: Delivering Final Results

Situation: You're presenting the completed aerial footage and photos to your client.

Practice: Focus on professional presentation and follow-up.

  • Presenting your work professionally
  • Explaining technical aspects simply
  • Asking for feedback
  • Discussing future projects
