Understanding CVCE Words

Learn how adding 'e' to the end of words changes their pronunciation

🎯 What is Magic E?

The "Magic E" rule helps you pronounce English words correctly. When you add an 'e' to the end of a word, it makes the vowel say its name (long sound) instead of its short sound.


Pattern: Consonant + Vowel + Consonant + E (CVCE)

cap
→
cape

cap /kæp/ = a hat

cape /keɪp/ = a cloak or piece of land

💬 Conversation Practice

Practice these conversation prompts with a partner or by yourself:

"My friend is very cute and nice. She always has hope for the future. Can you describe someone you know using CVCE words?"

Prompt 2: Daily Activities
