SUBJECT
The person or thing that DOES the action in a sentence.
Maria eats pizza.
Maria is the subject - she does the eating!
🏃‍♂️
VERB
The ACTION word! What someone or something DOES.
Maria eats pizza.
'Eats' is the verb - it's the action!
🎯
OBJECT
The person or thing that RECEIVES the action.
Maria eats pizza.
Pizza receives the action - it gets eaten!
🌈
ADJECTIVE
Words that DESCRIBE nouns. They tell us HOW something is.
The big dog is happy.
'Big' and 'happy' describe the dog!
🏠
NOUN
Names of people, places, animals, or things.
John lives in London.
'John' (person) and 'London' (place) are nouns!
📝
SENTENCE
A complete thought with a subject and verb.
The cat sleeps on the bed.
Subject + Verb + Object = Complete sentence!
