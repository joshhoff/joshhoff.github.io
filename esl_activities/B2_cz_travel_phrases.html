Travel Phrasal Verbs & Expressions

Set off / Set out

To begin a journey
Vydat se na cestu, začít cestu
"We set off early to avoid traffic." / "They set out for Prague at dawn."

Check in / Check out

To register arrival / To pay and leave a hotel
Přihlásit se / Odhlásit se (z hotelu)
"Please check in at reception." / "We need to check out by 11 AM."

Drop off / Pick up

To leave someone/something at a place / To collect
Vysadit, odvézt / Vyzvednout
"Can you drop me off at the airport?" / "I'll pick you up at 8 PM."

Get away

To escape or go on vacation
Uniknout, jet na dovolenou
"I need to get away from the city for a while."

Stop over

To make a brief stop during a journey
Udělat si zastávku, mezipřistání
"We'll stop over in Frankfurt on our way to Tokyo."

Look forward to

To anticipate with pleasure
Těšit se na
"I'm really looking forward to our trip to Italy."

Run into

To meet unexpectedly / To encounter problems
Narazit na, potkat náhodou
"We ran into some delays at customs." / "I ran into my old friend in Paris!"

Turn up

To arrive or appear unexpectedly
Objevit se, dorazit
"My luggage finally turned up after three days."

Branch out

To explore new destinations or try different experiences
Rozšířit obzory, zkusit něco nového
"This year we decided to branch out and visit Southeast Asia instead of Europe."

Fall through

When plans fail or don't happen
Nevyjít, ztroskotať
"Our vacation plans fell through when the airline went on strike."

Hold up

To delay or cause delays
Zdržet, způsobit zpoždění
"The security check held us up for over an hour."

Put up with

To tolerate unpleasant conditions
Snášet, tolerovat
"I can't put up with this noisy hotel room any longer."

Come across

To find or discover by chance
Narazit na, objevit náhodou
"We came across this amazing little restaurant in a back alley."

Get around

To travel from place to place / To avoid a problem
Pohybovat se, cestovat / Obejít problém
"It's easy to get around Prague by public transport." / "We found a way to get around the visa requirement."

Settle in

To become comfortable in a new place
Zabydlet se, usadit se
"It took us a few days to settle in at our new accommodation."

Map out

To plan in detail
Naplánovat detailně, rozvrhnout
"We spent hours mapping out our three-week European itinerary."

Touch down

When an aircraft lands
Přistát (letadlo)
"We're scheduled to touch down in Vienna at 14:30 local time."

See off

To accompany someone to their departure point
Doprovodit na odjezd, rozloučit se
"My family came to see me off at the airport."

Live it up

To enjoy yourself extravagantly
Užívat si naplno, žít si
"We decided to live it up on our last night in Paris."

Venture out

To go somewhere despite risks or uncertainty
Odvážit se vydat, riskovat cestu
"Despite the rain, we ventured out to explore the old town."

Stumble upon

To discover something unexpectedly
Narazit náhodou na, objevit neočekávaně
"We stumbled upon a hidden gem of a café while wandering through the streets."

Wind down

To relax and reduce activity
Uvolnit se, zklidnit se
"After a busy day of sightseeing, we like to wind down at a local pub."

Pull through

To successfully overcome difficulties
Překonat obtíže, zvládnout to
"Despite all the travel complications, we pulled through and had an amazing trip."

Conversation Practice

🏨 Scenario 1: Luxury Travel Complications

Situation: You've booked an expensive luxury resort, but your vacation plans are falling apart. The resort is overbooked, your spa treatments were cancelled, and you're considering legal action. Negotiate with the hotel manager for compensation.

Advanced challenges:
  • Demand appropriate compensation for the inconvenience
  • Negotiate alternative luxury accommodations
  • Discuss potential legal implications
  • Maintain professionalism while expressing frustration

Key phrases to use:

fall through put up with pull through hold up settle in

✈️ Scenario 2: Business Travel Crisis

Situation: You're a business executive whose international conference presentation has been jeopardized by travel complications. Your flight was cancelled, your presentation materials are lost, and you need to find creative solutions while maintaining professional relationships.

Professional challenges:
  • Explain the situation to important clients
  • Arrange emergency alternative travel
  • Coordinate with international colleagues across time zones
  • Salvage the business opportunity despite setbacks

Key phrases to use:

touch down branch out map out get around pull through

🗺️ Scenario 3: Adventure Travel Planning

Situation: You're organizing an extreme adventure expedition to remote locations with significant risks. Discuss insurance, emergency protocols, and contingency plans with your travel companions and expedition leader.

Complex considerations:
  • Risk assessment and safety protocols
  • Emergency evacuation procedures
  • Equipment failure contingencies
  • Cultural sensitivity in remote communities
  • Environmental impact and sustainability

Key phrases to use:

venture out map out stumble upon come across branch out pull through

🌍 Scenario 4: Cultural Immersion Dilemma

Situation: You're on a cultural exchange program, but you're struggling to adapt to local customs and experiencing culture shock. Discuss your challenges with your host family and program coordinator while being respectful of cultural differences.

Sensitive topics:
  • Addressing cultural misunderstandings diplomatically
  • Expressing homesickness without offending hosts
  • Negotiating personal boundaries respectfully
  • Finding balance between adaptation and authenticity

Key phrases to use:

settle in come across put up with wind down get around

🎉 Scenario 5: Milestone Celebration Travel

Situation: You're planning a once-in-a-lifetime celebration trip (anniversary, retirement, etc.) with a significant budget. Discuss luxury options, exclusive experiences, and creating unforgettable memories with a high-end travel consultant.

Luxury considerations:
  • Exclusive access to private venues and experiences
  • Personalized service and concierge arrangements
  • Balancing extravagance with meaningful experiences
  • Creating lasting memories within budget constraints

Key phrases to use:

live it up branch out stumble upon see off wind down
