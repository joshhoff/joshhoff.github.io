📖 Grammar Rules & Explanations

🔵 Present Simple

Form: Subject + base verb (+ s/es for 3rd person singular)

Use for:

  • Habits and routines
  • General truths and facts
  • Scheduled events
Examples:
• I work every day. (habit)
• The sun rises in the east. (fact)
• The train leaves at 9 AM. (schedule)
Diferencia con español: En español decimos "Trabajo todos los días" (presente simple). En inglés también usamos present simple para hábitos.

🔴 Present Perfect

Form: Subject + have/has + past participle

Use for:

  • Actions completed at an unspecified time in the past
  • Life experiences
  • Actions that started in the past and continue to the present
  • Recent actions with present relevance
Examples:
• I have visited Paris. (life experience)
• She has lived here for 5 years. (continues to present)
• They have just arrived. (recent action)
¡Cuidado! En español decimos "He visitado París" (pretérito perfecto compuesto) o "Visité París" (pretérito indefinido). En inglés, si no especificamos cuándo, usamos present perfect.

⚡ Key Differences

Present Simple Present Perfect
I work here. (general fact) I have worked here for 3 years. (duration)
She goes to Spain every year. (habit) She has been to Spain. (experience)

🚨 Common Mistakes for Spanish Speakers

❌ Incorrect: I am living here since 2020.

✅ Correct: I have lived here since 2020.

En español: "Vivo aquí desde 2020" - pero en inglés necesitamos present perfect para duración.

❌ Incorrect: I have breakfast every morning.

✅ Correct: I have breakfast every morning. (This is actually correct!)

Pero: "I have had breakfast" = Ya desayuné (present perfect)

💬 Conversation Practice

Practice using both tenses in realistic conversation contexts. Read the prompts and practice speaking or writing your responses.

🗣️ Situation 1: Job Interview

Interviewer: "Tell me about your work experience."

Your turn: Use present perfect to talk about your experience and present simple for your current job or habits.

Example response: "I have worked in marketing for five years. I have managed several successful campaigns. Currently, I work for a tech company where I handle social media every day."
🗣️ Situation 2: Meeting a Friend

Friend: "What's new with you?"

Your turn: Talk about recent experiences (present perfect) and current routines (present simple).

Example response: "I have just started a new hobby - photography! I take pictures every weekend now. I have already visited three different parks this month."
🗣️ Situation 3: Travel Discussion

Colleague: "Do you like to travel?"

Your turn: Discuss your travel experiences and preferences.

Example response: "Yes, I love traveling! I have been to fifteen countries so far. I usually travel twice a year. I have never been to Asia, but I really want to go there."
🗣️ Situation 4: Talking About Hobbies

New acquaintance: "What do you do in your free time?"

Your turn: Describe your regular activities and recent achievements.

Example response: "I play guitar and read books. I practice guitar every evening. I have learned five new songs this month. I have read twenty books this year!"
🗣️ Situation 5: Restaurant Experience

Waiter: "Have you decided what to order?"

Your turn: Talk about your food preferences and experiences.

Example response: "I usually order pasta, but I have never tried the seafood here. I have heard good things about the salmon. Do you recommend it?"

💡 Conversation Tips

  • Present Perfect: Use for experiences, recent actions, and unfinished time periods
  • Present Simple: Use for habits, routines, facts, and general truths
  • Time markers: "ever, never, just, already, yet" → Present Perfect
  • Time markers: "every day, usually, always, sometimes" → Present Simple
