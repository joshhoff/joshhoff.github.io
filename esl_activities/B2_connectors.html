💡 Natural Filler Word Tips:

  • "Well..." - Use when you need time to think or when changing topics
  • "So..." - Great for starting explanations or summaries
  • "You know..." - Helps check if your listener understands
  • "I mean..." - Perfect for clarifying or rephrasing
  • "Actually..." - Use when correcting or adding new information
