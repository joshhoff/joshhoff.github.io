Short /ʌ/ Sound

As in "cup", "run", "love"

🎯 How to Pronounce

  • •Open your mouth slightly
  • •Keep your tongue in the middle of your mouth
  • •Relax your jaw
  • •Make a short "uh" sound

Long /uː/ Sound

As in "blue", "true", "fruit"

🎯 How to Pronounce

  • •Round your lips into an "O" shape
  • •Pull your tongue back
  • •Make a long, sustained sound
  • •Like "oo" in "moon"

Short /ʊ/ Sound

As in "put", "book", "could"

🎯 How to Pronounce

  • •Lips slightly rounded
  • •Tongue high but relaxed
  • •Shorter than /uː/ sound
  • •Like "oo" but brief

📝 Click to Practice

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ Don't Say:

book /buːk/ (too long)

look /luːk/ (too long)

✅ Do Say:

book /bʊk/ (short and relaxed)

look /lʊk/ (short and relaxed)

/juː/ Sound

As in "use", "cute", "music"

🎯 How to Pronounce

  • •Start with "y" sound
  • •Tongue moves from high front to high back
  • •Glide into /uː/ sound
  • •Like "yoo" combined

Schwa /ə/ Sound

As in "about", "circus", "focus"

🎯 How to Pronounce

  • •Most relaxed vowel sound
  • •Neutral tongue position
  • •Very quick and unstressed
  • •The most common sound in English!

Silent "U"

As in "guard", "guess", "tongue"

🤫 Why is "U" Silent?

  • •Historical spelling from Old French
  • •The "u" was once pronounced
  • •Now it's just part of the spelling
  • •Focus on the sounds you DO hear

🧠 Memory Tricks

GU- words

Usually the "u" is silent after "g":

guard, guess, guide, guitar, guest

-AGUE words

The "u" is silent in these endings:

league, plague, vague, dialogue
