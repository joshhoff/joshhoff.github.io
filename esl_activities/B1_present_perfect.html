🎯 Introduction to Present Perfect

The present perfect tense is one of the most important tenses in English, especially in business and advertising contexts.

What you'll learn:

  • How to form and use the present perfect tense
  • When to use it in advertising and business contexts
  • Essential advertising vocabulary
  • Practical examples and exercises

This tense is perfect for describing achievements, experiences, and results - making it essential for advertising and marketing communications!

📖 How Present Perfect Works

Formation

Positive: Subject + have/has + past participle

Negative: Subject + haven't/hasn't + past participle

Question: Have/Has + subject + past participle?

Key Uses

  • Completed actions with present results
  • Life experiences (without specific time)
  • Recent actions with "just"
  • Actions continuing to present

⚠️ Important Signal Words:

already, just, yet, ever, never, since, for, recently, lately, so far

🎪 Present Perfect in Advertising

Why Advertisers Love Present Perfect

  • Shows Results: "We have helped over 1 million customers"
  • Creates Urgency: "Limited time - we have reduced prices by 50%"
  • Builds Trust: "Our brand has been trusted for 50 years"
  • Highlights Innovation: "We have developed breakthrough technology"

❌ Simple Past (Specific Time)

"We launched this product in 2020"

✅ Present Perfect (Ongoing Impact)

"We have launched revolutionary products"

💡 Real Advertising Examples

🏆 Achievement & Success

  • "We have won the Customer Service Award three years running"
  • "Our team has achieved record-breaking sales this quarter"
  • "The company has expanded to 50 countries worldwide"

🔄 Ongoing Benefits

  • "Customers have saved over $2 million with our service"
  • "We have been providing quality products since 1985"
  • "Our app has helped millions improve their productivity"

🆕 Recent Developments

  • "We have just launched our newest product line"
  • "The brand has recently partnered with leading retailers"
  • "Our prices have never been lower than today"

📢 Essential Advertising Vocabulary

Campaign Terms

  • Brand awareness - Recognition of a brand
  • Target audience - Intended customers
  • Market share - Portion of total sales
  • ROI - Return on investment

Sales Terms

  • Conversion rate - Visitors who buy
  • Lead generation - Finding potential customers
  • Customer retention - Keeping existing customers
  • Upselling - Selling premium products

Digital Terms

  • Click-through rate - Ad clicks vs views
  • Engagement - User interaction level
  • Viral marketing - Content that spreads rapidly
  • Influencer - Person who affects buying decisions

💡 Present Perfect + Advertising Vocab Examples:

  • "Our brand awareness has increased by 40% this year"
  • "We have improved our conversion rate significantly"
  • "The campaign has generated thousands of new leads"
  • "Our engagement has never been higher"
