📖 Reference Guide

👤 Person

Singular: person

"There is one person waiting."

Plural: people

"There are many people here."

👶 Child

Singular: child

"The child is playing."

Plural: children

"The children are laughing."

💡 Important Tips

  • Person → People: This is an irregular plural. Never say "persons" in everyday English.
  • Child → Children: Another irregular plural. The word "childs" doesn't exist.
  • Verb Agreement: Use "is" with singular forms and "are" with plural forms.
  • Articles: Use "a" or "an" with singular forms, no article needed for plural forms when speaking generally.

Common Usage Examples

✅ Correct: "That person is very kind."
❌ Incorrect: "That people is very kind."
✅ Correct: "Those people are waiting."
❌ Incorrect: "Those person are waiting."
✅ Correct: "Every child needs love."
❌ Incorrect: "Every children needs love."
✅ Correct: "All children deserve happiness."
❌ Incorrect: "All child deserve happiness."
