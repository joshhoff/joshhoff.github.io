🕵️ Detective English Practice

Learn vocabulary and grammar with detective stories!

🔍 Clue
A piece of information that helps solve a mystery or problem.
"The detective found a clue at the crime scene."
🕵️ Suspect
A person who might have done something wrong.
"The police questioned the suspect about the missing jewels."
📷 Camera
A device that takes pictures or records videos.
"The security camera recorded everything that happened."
🔓 Lock Pick
A special tool used to open locks without a key.
"The thief used a lock pick to enter the building."
🦀 Claw
The sharp, curved nail of an animal like a cat or crab.
"The cat used its claw to climb the tree."
🐧 Flipper
The flat body part that sea animals use to swim.
"The penguin used its flipper to swim fast in the water."
📁 Folder
A container that holds papers or documents together.
"Agent Folder keeps all the case files organized."
👁️ Witness
A person who saw something happen.
"The witness told the police what she saw that night."

Boggy's looks over time.
Past Tense: "Changed" is correct! It shows something that happened before now. Boggy looked different in the past than now.
