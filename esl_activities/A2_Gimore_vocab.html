🕵️ Detective English Practice

Learn vocabulary and grammar with detective stories!

🤔 Suspicious
Acting in a way that makes people think something is wrong.
"The man looked suspicious near the bank."
💰 Bribe
Money given to someone to make them do something dishonest.
"The guard accepted a bribe to let them in."
📈 Profit
Money earned from selling something for more than it cost.
"The store made a good profit this month."
🧳 Tourist
A person who visits a place for pleasure or vacation.
"Many tourists visit the museum every day."
🛍️ Gift Shop
A store that sells souvenirs and small presents.
"We bought postcards at the gift shop."
🏠 Tree House
A small house built in the branches of a tree.
"The children played in their tree house."
🌉 Bridge
A structure built over water or roads to help people cross.
"We walked across the old wooden bridge."
🎭 Costume
Special clothes worn to look like someone or something else.
"She wore a ghost costume for Halloween."
🪟 Curtain
Fabric that hangs in front of windows or stages.
"Please close the curtain to block the sun."
👤 Appearance
How someone or something looks.
"His appearance changed after the haircut."
