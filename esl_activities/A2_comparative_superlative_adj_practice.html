Quick Grammar Refresher:

  • Comparative: Compares two things (bigger, more expensive)
  • Superlative: Compares three or more things (biggest, most expensive)
  • Short adjectives: Add -er/-est (fast → faster → fastest)
  • Long adjectives: Use more/most (expensive → more expensive → most expensive)
