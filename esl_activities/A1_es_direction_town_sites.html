📍 Directions Vocabulary

Basic Directions

  • Left - to the left side
  • Right - to the right side
  • Straight - continue forward
  • Turn - change direction
  • Go - move forward
  • Walk - move on foot

Position Words

  • Next to - beside, adjacent
  • Between - in the middle of two things
  • Across from - on the opposite side
  • Behind - at the back of
  • In front of - at the front of
  • Near - close to

Advanced Positions

  • To the right of - on the right side of
  • To the left of - on the left side of
  • On the corner - at the intersection
  • At the end of - at the final point
  • Around the corner - just past the turn
  • Down the street - further along
