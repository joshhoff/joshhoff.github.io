Master English Adjective Order

For Advanced ESL Learners

Welcome to Adjective Order Mastery

As an advanced ESL learner, you've likely noticed that native English speakers seem to instinctively know the "right" order for multiple adjectives. This isn't magic—it's a systematic pattern that you can master!

Why Does Adjective Order Matter?

  • • It makes your English sound more natural and fluent
  • • It helps avoid confusion in meaning
  • • It's essential for advanced academic and professional communication

✓ Sounds Natural:

"A beautiful small Italian leather handbag"

✗ Sounds Awkward:

"A leather Italian small beautiful handbag"

The OSASCOMP Rule

O-S-A-S-C-O-M-P

The magic formula for adjective order!

O

Opinion

beautiful, ugly, nice, terrible, wonderful

S

Size

big, small, tiny, huge, enormous

A

Age

old, new, young, ancient, modern

S

Shape

round, square, flat, curved, straight

C

Color

red, blue, green, black, white

O

Origin

American, Chinese, European, local

M

Material

wooden, plastic, metal, cotton, silk

P

Purpose

sleeping (bag), running (shoes), cooking (pot)

💡 Pro Tip

You don't need to use all categories! Most phrases use 2-3 adjectives maximum. The key is maintaining the correct order when you do use multiple adjectives.

Conversation Practice

Speaking Prompts

Practice using correct adjective order in natural conversation contexts.

Prompt 1: Describing Your Dream Home

Imagine you're describing your dream home to a friend. Use at least 3 different adjectives for each room or feature you mention. Pay attention to adjective order!

Example start:

"I'd love to have a beautiful large modern kitchen with..."

✓ Good Phrases to Practice:

  • • "A cozy small wooden cabin"
  • • "Beautiful old Italian marble floors"
  • • "A spacious modern American kitchen"
  • • "Comfortable new leather furniture"

💭 Think About:

  • • What's your opinion of each feature?
  • • How big or small is it?
  • • Is it old or new?
  • • What material is it made of?

💡 Conversation Hints

  • • Start with your opinion (beautiful, awful, perfect)
  • • Add size if relevant (huge, tiny, spacious)
  • • Include age when describing character (old, new, vintage)
  • • Mention materials for texture (wooden, marble, steel)
