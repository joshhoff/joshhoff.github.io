Quick Review: Connector Words

Connector words join ideas and show relationships between sentences.

Time Connectors

First, then, next, finally, meanwhile, subsequently

Contrast Connectors

However, nevertheless, although, despite, on the other hand

Cause & Effect

Therefore, consequently, as a result, due to, because of

Addition Connectors

Furthermore, moreover, in addition, also, as well as

Quick Review: Phrasal Verbs

Phrasal verbs combine a verb with a preposition or adverb to create a new meaning.

Examples: set up, look into, follow up, go through, put off
