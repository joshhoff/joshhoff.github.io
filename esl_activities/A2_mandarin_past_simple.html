Simple Past Tense

过去时态学习 (Past Tense Learning)

What is Simple Past? 什么是一般过去时？

English 英语

The simple past tense describes actions that happened and finished in the past.

Examples:

• I walked to school yesterday.

• She ate lunch at 12 PM.

• They watched a movie last night.

Chinese 中文

一般过去时描述在过去发生并已完成的动作。

例子:

• 我昨天走路去学校。

• 她12点吃了午饭。

• 他们昨晚看了电影。

Key Difference 主要区别:

In Chinese, you often add 了 (le) to show past actions. In English, you change the verb form (walk → walked).

中文中，你经常加"了"来表示过去的动作。英语中，你要改变动词形式。

Grammar Rules 语法规则

1. Regular Verbs 规则动词

Add -ed to the base form 在动词原形后加-ed

Most verbs 大多数动词

walk → walked

play → played

work → worked

Verbs ending in -e 以-e结尾

live → lived

dance → danced

smile → smiled

Consonant + y 辅音+y

study → studied

try → tried

cry → cried

3. Time Expressions 时间表达

Common time words 常用时间词

  • • yesterday 昨天
  • • last week/month/year 上周/上月/去年
  • • ago (2 hours ago) 以前
  • • in 2020 在2020年
  • • when I was young 当我年轻时

Example sentences 例句

  • • I studied yesterday.
  • • She visited China last year.
  • • We met two days ago.
  • • He lived here in 2019.
  • • They played soccer when they were kids.

Examples 例句

Daily Activities 日常活动

I woke up at 7 AM.

我早上7点醒了。

She cooked dinner for her family.

她为家人做了晚饭。

We watched TV after work.

我们下班后看了电视。

They went shopping yesterday.

他们昨天去购物了。

He studied English for 2 hours.

他学了2小时英语。

I called my mother last night.

我昨晚给妈妈打了电话。

Past Experiences 过去经历

I visited Beijing when I was a student.

我当学生时去过北京。

She learned to drive last year.

她去年学会了开车。

We moved to this city five years ago.

我们五年前搬到这个城市。

Conversation Practice 对话练习

Conversation Prompts 对话提示

Practice these conversations with a partner or teacher 与伙伴或老师练习这些对话

Topic 1: Weekend Activities 周末活动

A: What did you do last weekend?

B: I went to the park and played basketball. What about you?

A: I stayed home and watched movies.

Your turn! Talk about:

  • • Where you went 你去了哪里
  • • What you ate 你吃了什么
  • • Who you met 你见了谁
  • • How you felt 你感觉如何

Topic 2: Yesterday's Schedule 昨天的日程

A: How was your day yesterday?

B: It was busy! I woke up early, went to work, and had three meetings.

A: That sounds tiring! What did you do after work?

B: I cooked dinner and called my family.

Practice questions:

  • • What time did you wake up? 你几点醒的？
  • • Where did you go? 你去了哪里？
  • • What did you have for lunch? 你午饭吃了什么？
  • • Did you exercise yesterday? 你昨天运动了吗？

Topic 3: Past Experiences 过去经历

A: Have you ever been to Japan?

B: Yes, I visited Tokyo two years ago. I loved the food!

A: What was your favorite place there?

B: I really enjoyed the temples and gardens.

Share your experiences:

  • • A place you visited 你去过的地方
  • • A skill you learned 你学过的技能
  • • A memorable event 难忘的事件
  • • Your first job 你的第一份工作
