Advanced Article Rules

Definite Article "THE"

Specific items: "The book you recommended" / "Книга, яку ти рекомендував" / "El libro que recomendaste"

Unique items: "The sun, the moon" / "Сонце, місяць" / "El sol, la luna"

Superlatives: "The best solution" / "Найкраще рішення" / "La mejor solución"

Indefinite Articles "A/AN"

First mention: "I saw a movie" / "Я подивився фільм" / "Vi una película"

One of many: "She's a teacher" / "Вона вчителька" / "Ella es profesora"

Per/each: "Once a week" / "Раз на тиждень" / "Una vez por semana"

Zero Article (No Article)

General concepts: "Life is beautiful" / "Життя прекрасне" / "La vida es hermosa"

Plural generalizations: "Dogs are loyal" / "Собаки вірні" / "Los perros son leales"

Proper nouns: "Paris, John" / "Париж, Джон" / "París, Juan"

Advanced Challenges

Idiomatic Expressions

Play ___ piano vs. play ___ football
Answer: Play THE piano, play ∅ football
Musical instruments use "the", sports use no article

Abstract Concepts

___ love conquers all vs. ___ love I feel for you
Answer: ∅ Love conquers all, THE love I feel for you
General concept vs. specific instance

Context-Dependent Usage

Go to ___ hospital (as patient) vs. go to ___ hospital (to visit)
Answer: Go to ∅ hospital (patient), go to THE hospital (visitor)
Purpose vs. specific location
