Modal Verbs for Spanish Speakers

Learn English modal verbs by connecting them to Spanish equivalents you already know!

What are Modal Verbs?

🎯 Function

Modal verbs are helper verbs that show:

  • Possibility - might, may, could
  • Ability - can, could
  • Permission - can, may
  • Obligation - must, should

🔗 Spanish Connection

Similar to Spanish verbs you know:

  • poder → can, could
  • deber → must, should
  • querer → would
  • soler → would (habits)

Key Rules

Never Change Form

I can, she can, we can
❌ cans, canned

Base Form After

can go, should study
❌ can going

No "Do" Needed

Can you swim?
❌ Do you can swim?
