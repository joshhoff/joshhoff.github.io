What are reflexive verbs?

Reflexive verbs are used when the subject and object of a verb are the same person. In English, we use reflexive pronouns (myself, yourself, etc.). In Spanish, reflexive verbs use 'se' in their infinitive form and reflexive pronouns (me, te, se, etc.).

Reflexive Verbs in Spanish

Many daily routine verbs in Spanish are reflexive. They use reflexive pronouns:

  • me (myself) - Me despierto (I wake up)
  • te (yourself) - Te despiertas (You wake up)
  • se (himself/herself/itself) - Se despierta (He/She wakes up)
  • nos (ourselves) - Nos despertamos (We wake up)
  • os (yourselves) - Os despertáis (You all wake up - Spain)
  • se (themselves) - Se despiertan (They wake up)

Daily Routine Examples

Morning Routine

English

I wake up at 6:30 AM every day. First, I stretch in bed for a few minutes. Then I get up and make my bed. I go to the bathroom where I brush my teeth and wash my face. After that, I take a quick shower. I dry myself with a towel and get dressed for work. Before leaving home, I have breakfast - usually coffee and toast. I leave home at 8:00 AM and go to work by bus.

Spanish

Me despierto a las 6:30 AM todos los días. Primero, me estiro en la cama por unos minutos. Luego me levanto y hago mi cama. Voy al baño donde me cepillo los dientes y me lavo la cara. Después de eso, me ducho rápidamente. Me seco con una toalla y me visto para el trabajo. Antes de salir de casa, desayuno - generalmente café y tostadas. Salgo de casa a las 8:00 AM y voy al trabajo en autobús.

Evening Routine

English

I come home from work at around 6:00 PM. I change into comfortable clothes and relax for a while. I prepare dinner at 7:00 PM and eat with my family. After dinner, I wash the dishes and clean the kitchen. I usually watch TV or read a book for an hour. Before bed, I take a warm shower and brush my teeth. I set my alarm for the next day. Finally, I go to bed at 10:30 PM and fall asleep quickly.

Spanish

Vuelvo a casa del trabajo alrededor de las 6:00 PM. Me cambio a ropa cómoda y me relajo por un rato. Preparo la cena a las 7:00 PM y como con mi familia. Después de cenar, lavo los platos y limpio la cocina. Normalmente veo la televisión o leo un libro por una hora. Antes de acostarme, me ducho con agua tibia y me cepillo los dientes. Pongo la alarma para el día siguiente. Finalmente, me acuesto a las 10:30 PM y me duermo rápidamente.

Weekend Routine

English

On weekends, I wake up later, usually at 8:00 AM. I don't rush to get up. I take my time to stretch and relax in bed. Then I make myself a special breakfast - pancakes or eggs. After breakfast, I get dressed in casual clothes. I often go shopping or meet friends for coffee. In the afternoon, I clean my apartment and do laundry. In the evening, I sometimes go out for dinner or watch a movie at home. I go to bed around midnight on weekends.

Spanish

Los fines de semana, me despierto más tarde, normalmente a las 8:00 AM. No me apresuro a levantarme. Me tomo mi tiempo para estirarme y relajarme en la cama. Luego me preparo un desayuno especial - panqueques o huevos. Después del desayuno, me visto con ropa casual. A menudo voy de compras o me encuentro con amigos para tomar café. Por la tarde, limpio mi apartamento y lavo la ropa. Por la noche, a veces salgo a cenar o veo una película en casa. Me acuesto alrededor de la medianoche los fines de semana.

Role-Play Scenarios

Practice these conversations with a partner or by yourself, taking both roles.

Scenario 1: Morning Routine

Person A:

Good morning! You look tired. What time did you wake up today?

Person B:

I woke up very early, at 5:30 AM. I couldn't fall back asleep.

Person A:

That's too early! What did you do after you got up?

Person B:

I took a shower, got dressed, and had breakfast. Then I read a book until it was time to leave for work.

Person A:

That sounds nice. What time do you usually wake up?

Person B:

I usually wake up at 7:00 AM. What about you? What's your morning routine like?

Scenario 2: Weekend Plans

Person A:

Do you have any plans for this weekend?

Person B:

Yes, I'm going to visit my parents. I'll wake up early on Saturday and drive to their house.

Person A:

That sounds nice. What will you do there?

Person B:

We'll have lunch together, and then maybe watch a movie or go for a walk. What about you? What are your weekend plans?

Person A:

I don't have any special plans. I'll probably sleep in, have a late breakfast, and then clean my apartment. Maybe I'll go shopping in the afternoon.
