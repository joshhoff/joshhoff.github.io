The Magic E Rule

What is the Magic E?

When you add an 'e' to the end of a word, it changes the vowel sound from short to long. The 'e' is silent but magical!


Pattern: Consonant + Vowel + Consonant + E

cap
/kæp/
→
cape
/keɪp/
bit
/bɪt/
→
bite
/baɪt/
cut
/kʌt/
→
cute
/kjuːt/

Present Tense Formation

Simple Present Tense Rules:

• I, you, we, they → use base form

• He, she, it → add -s or -es


Special cases:

• Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z → add -es

• Verbs ending in consonant + y → change y to -ies

WORK
I work
He works
WATCH
I watch
She watches
STUDY
I study
He studies
HAVE
I have
She has

Practice Exercises

Question 1: Which word has the long 'a' sound?

cat
cake
cap
can

Correct! "Cake" has the magic e, which makes the 'a' say its name: /keɪk/

Question 2: Complete: "She _____ to school every day." (go)

go
goes
going
went

Correct! With "she" (third person singular), we add -es to "go" because it ends in -o.

Question 3: Which pair shows the magic e rule?

cat → cats
hop → hope
run → running
big → bigger

Correct! "Hop" /hɒp/ becomes "hope" /hoʊp/ - the magic e changes the vowel sound!

Question 4: Complete: "My brother _____ his homework every evening." (do)

do
does
did
doing

Correct! "Brother" is third person singular (he), so we use "does".

Question 5: Which word has the magic e making the 'i' long?

sit
big
bike
pick

Correct! "Bike" /baɪk/ has the magic e that makes the 'i' say its name!

Question 6: Complete: "They _____ French and English." (speak)

speak
speaks
speaking
spoke

Correct! "They" uses the base form of the verb without adding -s.

Conversation Practice

Practice these common situations using present tense and magic e words!

🏠 At Home

Situation: Describe your daily routine

You: "I wake up at seven. I make breakfast and ride my bike to work."
Friend: "That's nice! What time do you arrive at work?"
You: "I arrive at nine. My boss likes when we come on time."

🛒 Shopping

Situation: At the grocery store

Cashier: "Do you have a store card?"
You: "Yes, I use it every time I shop here."
Cashier: "Great! That saves you five dollars today."

👥 Meeting People

Situation: Introducing yourself

New Person: "Hi! What's your name?"
You: "I'm Marie. I come from France but I live here now."
New Person: "Nice to meet you! Do you like it here?"
You: "Yes! People are nice and I make new friends easily."
