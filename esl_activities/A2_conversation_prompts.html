At the Pool

You're at a public swimming pool. Practice conversations about swimming, water safety, and recreational activities.

attendant

Welcome to the community pool. Have you been here before?

attendant

The changing rooms are over there, and the lockers require a quarter to use. Do you need anything else?

lifeguard

Just a reminder that there's no running around the pool area, and children under 12 need adult supervision.

swimmer

Excuse me, do you know if they offer swimming lessons here?

How would you respond to another swimmer?

Suggested responses (or create your own):
I'm not sure, you should ask the lifeguard.
Yes, I think they have classes on weekends.
I don't know, I'm new here too.

Vocabulary

lifeguard

A person who supervises swimmers and is trained to rescue people in danger of drowning

shallow end

The part of a swimming pool where the water is not deep

deep end

The part of a swimming pool where the water is deep

lane

A marked section of a pool for swimming laps

diving board

A board from which people jump into water

sunscreen

A lotion that protects skin from the sun

float

To stay on the surface of water without sinking

sunbathe

To sit or lie in the sun to get a tan
