B2 tip: meaning can change when a verb takes an object.

01 / Grammar reference

Find the object. Find the pattern.

Select a card to open the note.

A transitive verb needs or can take a direct object: someone or something receives the action. Ask “what?” or “whom?” after the verb.

They discussed the proposal for over an hour. → Discussed what? The proposal.

An intransitive verb does not take a direct object. The sentence can be complete after the verb, though it may include time, place, or manner.

The audience laughed at the joke. → “At the joke” is a prepositional phrase, not a direct object.

04 / Speak it

Conversation prompts

Prompt 1

Prompt 2
