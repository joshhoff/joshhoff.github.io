🎯 Participial Adjectives in Context

Learn when and why to use -ed vs -ing adjectives

🧠 Understanding Context & Usage

🔑 The Context Rule:

Ask yourself: "What am I describing?"

  • Describing a PERSON's emotional state? → Use -ED
  • Describing a THING's effect on people? → Use -ING
👤 -ED: Describing People's States
When to use: The person/animal experiences the emotion
Sarah is frustrated with her computer.
→ Sarah experiences frustration
The students were confused by the instructions.
→ Students experienced confusion
I am excited about the concert tonight.
→ I experience excitement
📱 -ING: Describing Things' Effects
When to use: The thing/situation causes the emotion
This computer is frustrating to use.
→ Computer causes frustration
The instructions were confusing and unclear.
→ Instructions cause confusion
Tonight's concert will be exciting!
→ Concert causes excitement

💡 Context Clues to Look For:

Use -ED when the subject is:

  • A person: "John is tired"
  • An animal: "The cat looks scared"
  • A group of people: "We are interested"

Use -ING when the subject is:

  • An activity: "Swimming is relaxing"
  • An object: "This book is boring"
  • A situation: "The weather is depressing"

💬 Contextual Conversation Practice

🎭 Role-Play Scenarios:

Scenario 1: Movie Review

Context: You and your friend are discussing a movie you both watched.

Practice: Describe both how you felt (use -ed) and what the movie was like (use -ing)

Example: "I was really surprised by the ending, but the movie was quite surprising overall."

Scenario 2: Work/School Day

Context: Talking about your day with family or friends.

Practice: Describe your feelings and what caused them

Example: "I felt exhausted because the meeting was exhausting."

Scenario 3: Travel Experience

Context: Sharing your travel experience with someone.

Practice: Talk about places (use -ing) and your reactions (use -ed)

Example: "The museum was fascinating, so I was fascinated by the exhibits."

🗣️ Discussion Questions:

  1. Think of something that happened today. How did you feel? What caused that feeling?
  2. Describe a boring activity and explain why you get bored doing it.
  3. What's the most exciting thing you've done recently? Why were you excited?
  4. Tell about a time when you were confused. What was confusing about the situation?
