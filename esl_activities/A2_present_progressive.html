📚 Grammar Lessons

Lesson 1: Present Progressive (Present Continuous)

📝 What is Present Progressive?

We use Present Progressive to talk about actions happening RIGHT NOW or temporary situations.

🔧 How to make it:

Formula: Subject + am/is/are + verb + -ing

Positive: I am working | You/We/They are working | He/She/It is working

Negative: I am not working | You/We/They are not working | He/She/It is not working

Question: Am I working? | Are you/we/they working? | Is he/she/it working?

💡 Examples:

✓ I am studying English right now.

✓ She is working on her computer.

✓ They are not watching TV today.

⏰ Time expressions we use:

now • right now • at the moment • today • this week • currently

Lesson 2: Stative Verbs (Verbs We DON'T Use in Progressive)

📝 What are Stative Verbs?

Stative verbs describe states, feelings, thoughts, or senses. We usually DON'T use them with -ing.

🧠 Categories of Stative Verbs:

Feelings & Emotions: love, like, hate, want, need, prefer

Thinking & Knowing: know, understand, believe, think (opinion), remember

Senses: see, hear, smell, taste, feel

Possession & Being: have (own), be, belong, own, contain

✅ Correct vs ❌ Incorrect:

✅ I know the answer. (NOT: I am knowing)

✅ She loves chocolate. (NOT: She is loving)

✅ This soup tastes good. (NOT: This soup is tasting)

Lesson 3: Common Verb Combinations

📝 What are Verb Combinations?

Some verbs work together in special ways. Here are the most common patterns:

🔗 Common Patterns:

Verb + to + infinitive: want to go, need to study, like to read

Example: I want to learn English.

Verb + -ing: enjoy doing, finish working, keep trying

Example: She enjoys reading books.

Modal + base verb: can speak, should study, must go

Example: You should practice every day.

📚 Common Verb + to + infinitive:

want • need • like • love • hate • hope • plan • decide • try • learn • forget • remember

📚 Common Verb + -ing:

enjoy • finish • stop • keep • avoid • practice • suggest • mind • consider

💬 Conversation Practice

Speaking Prompts

Practice these conversation starters. Try to speak for 1-2 minutes on each topic:

🏠 Topic 1: Your Home

Describe your home. Where do you live? How many rooms does it have? What's your favorite room and why?

🍕 Topic 2: Food & Cooking

What's your favorite food? Can you cook? Describe a typical meal in your country.

🎯 Topic 3: Hobbies & Free Time

What do you like to do in your free time? Do you have any hobbies? What did you do last weekend?

🎭 Role Play Scenarios

Practice asking for prices, finding items, and making purchases.

🍽️ At a Restaurant
