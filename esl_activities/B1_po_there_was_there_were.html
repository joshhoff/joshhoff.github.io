📚 Zasada Gramatyczna / Grammar Rule

THERE WAS (był/była/było)

Używamy z rzeczownikami w liczbie pojedynczej:

  • • There was a book on the table.
  • • There was one student in class.
  • • There was some milk in the fridge.

THERE WERE (były/było)

Używamy z rzeczownikami w liczbie mnogiej:

  • • There were books on the table.
  • • There were many students in class.
  • • There were two cats in the garden.
