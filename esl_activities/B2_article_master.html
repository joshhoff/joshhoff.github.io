Quick Rules

Definite Article (the)

Used for specific nouns that are known to both speaker and listener.

Indefinite Articles (a/an)

'A' before consonant sounds, 'an' before vowel sounds. Used for non-specific or first-mention nouns.

Zero Article (∅)

No article used with plural/uncountable nouns in general statements, or with some proper nouns.
