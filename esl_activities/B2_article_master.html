Article Usage Guidelines

Definite Article (the)

Used for specific entities known to both parties in the communication. Example: "The quarterly report shows significant growth."

Indefinite Articles (a/an)

'A' before consonant sounds, 'an' before vowel sounds. Used for non-specific or first-mention entities. Example: "We need an effective strategy to address this issue."

Zero Article (∅)

No article used with plural/uncountable nouns in general statements, or with some proper nouns. Example: "∅ Productivity increased after ∅ implementation of new systems."
