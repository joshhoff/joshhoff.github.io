Simple Present Tense Rules

✅ Affirmative Form

Structure: Subject + Verb (+ s/es for 3rd person singular)

Examples:

  • I play soccer.
  • She plays soccer.
  • They work hard.
  • He watches TV.

Remember: Add -s or -es to verbs with he/she/it!

❌ Negative Form

Structure: Subject + do/does + not + base verb

Examples:

  • I do not (don't) like coffee.
  • She does not (doesn't) speak French.
  • We do not (don't) work on Sundays.
  • It does not (doesn't) rain much here.

Remember: Use "does not" with he/she/it, "do not" with I/you/we/they!

❓ Interrogative Form

Structure: Do/Does + subject + base verb + ?

Examples:

  • Do you like pizza?
  • Does she speak English?
  • Do they live here?
  • Does it work properly?

Remember: Use "Does" with he/she/it, "Do" with I/you/we/they!
