Help & Tips

Positive vs. Negative Forms

In English, we form negatives by adding "not" or using words like "no," "nothing," "nowhere."

Key Rules to Remember

  • Use don't/doesn't/didn't with base form verbs
  • After negatives, use any instead of some
  • Avoid double negatives (unlike many other languages)

Examples

✓ I don't have any money.

✗ I don't have no money.
