Understanding Modal Verbs

Modal verbs are auxiliary verbs that express necessity, possibility, permission, or ability. At C1 level, you should be able to use modal verbs with precision and understand their nuanced meanings.

Possibility & Probability

  • may, might, could
  • can, will, must
  • should, ought to

Obligation & Necessity

  • must, have to, need to
  • should, ought to
  • had better

Permission & Ability

  • can, could, may
  • be able to
  • be allowed to

Modal Perfect Forms

At C1 level, you should master modal perfect forms (modal + have + past participle) to express past possibilities or speculations:

Example: She must have forgotten about our meeting. (past deduction)

Example: They could have won if they had tried harder. (past possibility)

Modal Verbs in Context

Expressing Degrees of Certainty

Strong Certainty

  • Must: "This must be the right address; it matches the description perfectly."
  • Can't/Couldn't: "She can't be serious about quitting her job."
  • Will: "The project will definitely succeed with this team."

Medium to Low Certainty

  • Should: "The package should arrive by tomorrow."
  • May/Might: "The weather forecast might change before the weekend."
  • Could: "This approach could work, but I'm not entirely convinced."

Advanced Modal Structures

Structure Example Meaning
Modal + be + -ing "They must be waiting for us at the restaurant." Speculation about ongoing actions
Modal + have + been + -ing "She could have been working on the project all night." Speculation about past continuous actions
Modal + passive "The documents should be signed by the end of the week." Modal meaning with passive voice
Modal + perfect passive "The mistake could have been avoided with proper planning." Past possibility in passive voice

Modal Verbs in Professional Contexts

Business Email Example

Dear Ms. Johnson,

Thank you for your inquiry. We would be delighted to schedule a meeting next week. I should be able to provide all the information you requested by then. If you could let me know your availability, I will arrange everything accordingly.

Please note that our CEO might join us, depending on his schedule. In that case, we may need to adjust the time slightly.

I look forward to your response.

Best regards,
David Chen
