Present Continuous Tense

Presente Progressivo

📚 Introduction

The Present Continuous tense describes actions happening right now or temporary situations. It's one of the most important tenses for everyday English conversation!

💡 Key Point: We use this tense for actions in progress at the moment of speaking.

📚 Introduzione

Il Presente Progressivo descrive azioni che stanno accadendo in questo momento o situazioni temporanee. È uno dei tempi più importanti per la conversazione inglese quotidiana!

💡 Punto chiave: Usiamo questo tempo per azioni in corso nel momento in cui parliamo.

🔧 How to Form Present Continuous

✅ Positive Form

Subject + am/is/are + verb + ing

• I am working

• She is reading

• They are playing

❌ Negative Form

Subject + am/is/are + not + verb + ing

• I am not working

• She isn't reading

• They aren't playing

❓ Question Form

Am/Is/Are + subject + verb + ing?

• Are you working?

• Is she reading?

• Are they playing?

🔧 Come Formare il Presente Progressivo

✅ Forma Affermativa

Soggetto + am/is/are + verbo + ing

• I am working (sto lavorando)

• She is reading (sta leggendo)

• They are playing (stanno giocando)

❌ Forma Negativa

Soggetto + am/is/are + not + verbo + ing

• I am not working (non sto lavorando)

• She isn't reading (non sta leggendo)

• They aren't playing (non stanno giocando)

❓ Forma Interrogativa

Am/Is/Are + soggetto + verbo + ing?

• Are you working? (stai lavorando?)

• Is she reading? (sta leggendo?)

• Are they playing? (stanno giocando?)

⚠️ Important Exception: Stative Verbs

Some verbs describe states rather than actions. These verbs are usually NOT used in present continuous.

🧠 Mental States

  • • know
  • • understand
  • • believe
  • • remember
  • • think (opinion)

❤️ Emotions

  • • love
  • • hate
  • • like
  • • want
  • • need

👁️ Senses

  • • see
  • • hear
  • • smell
  • • taste
  • • feel

Examples:

❌ I am knowing the answer

✅ I know the answer

❌ She is loving pizza

✅ She loves pizza

⚠️ Eccezione Importante: Verbi di Stato

Alcuni verbi descrivono stati piuttosto che azioni. Questi verbi di solito NON si usano nel presente progressivo.

🧠 Stati Mentali

  • • know (sapere)
  • • understand (capire)
  • • believe (credere)
  • • remember (ricordare)
  • • think (pensare/opinione)

❤️ Emozioni

  • • love (amare)
  • • hate (odiare)
  • • like (piacere)
  • • want (volere)
  • • need (aver bisogno)

👁️ Sensi

  • • see (vedere)
  • • hear (sentire)
  • • smell (odorare)
  • • taste (assaggiare)
  • • feel (sentire)

Esempi:

❌ I am knowing the answer

✅ I know the answer

❌ She is loving pizza

✅ She loves pizza
