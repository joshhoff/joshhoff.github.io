Master Present Simple & Present Perfect Tenses

Present Simple Tense

Use: For habits, facts, and general truths

Structure: Subject + base verb (+ s/es for 3rd person singular)

  • I work every day
  • She speaks three languages
  • The sun rises in the east
  • Do you like coffee?
  • He doesn't play tennis

Present Perfect Tense

Use: For actions that started in the past and continue to the present, or completed actions with present relevance

Structure: Subject + have/has + past participle

  • I have lived here for five years
  • She has finished her homework
  • We have never been to Japan
  • Have you seen this movie?
  • He hasn't called me yet

💬 Conversation Practice Prompts

🏠 Daily Routines (Present Simple)

Practice talking about:

• What time do you usually wake up?

• What do you do on weekends?

• How often do you exercise?

• Where do you work/study?

Example: "I wake up at 7 AM every day. I usually have breakfast and then go to work."

🌟 Life Experiences (Present Perfect)

Practice talking about:

• Have you ever traveled to another country?

• What's the most interesting book you have read?

• Have you learned any new skills recently?

• What changes have you made in your life this year?

Example: "I have visited five countries so far. The most beautiful place I have been to is Japan."

🎯 Goals and Achievements (Mixed Tenses)

Practice talking about:

• What do you want to achieve this year?

• What have you accomplished recently?

• How do you usually set your goals?

• What progress have you made on your current projects?

Example: "I usually set three main goals each year. This year, I have already completed two of them."

🍽️ Food and Cooking (Mixed Tenses)

Practice talking about:

• What do you usually eat for breakfast?

• Have you tried any new restaurants lately?

• How often do you cook at home?

• What's the best dish you have ever made?

Example: "I cook dinner almost every night. Recently, I have learned how to make Italian pasta from scratch."

📱 Technology and Hobbies (Mixed Tenses)

Practice talking about:

• How do you use technology in your daily life?

• What new apps have you downloaded recently?

• What hobbies do you enjoy?

• Have you started any new hobbies this year?

Example: "I use my phone for everything. I have recently started learning photography as a new hobby."

💡 Conversation Tips

Present Simple: Use for habits, routines, and general facts

Present Perfect: Use for experiences, recent actions, and actions that continue to the present

Practice tip: Try to use both tenses in your responses to create more natural conversations!
