🎯 Present Perfect

Used for actions that started in the past and continue to the present, or have relevance now.

✅ I have lived here for 5 years.
✅ She has just finished her homework.
✅ We have never been to Japan.

⏰ Past Perfect

Used for actions that were completed before another past action or time.

✅ I had eaten before you arrived.
✅ They had already left when we got there.
✅ She had studied English before moving to Canada.
🎉 Fun Fact: Perfect tenses help you sound more natural and precise in English! ✨
