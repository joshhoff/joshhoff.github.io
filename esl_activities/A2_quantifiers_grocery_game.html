Learning Quantifiers

Understanding how to use "a few," "a little," "many," and "much" correctly in English.

A Few

Used with countable nouns (things you can count). It means a small number.

I have a few apples.

There are a few customers in the store.

She bought a few bananas.

A Little

Used with uncountable nouns (things you cannot count). It means a small amount.

I need a little water.

There's a little milk left in the fridge.

She has a little money to spend.

Many

Used with countable nouns. It means a large number.

There are many customers today.

How many apples do you want?

She has many friends at school.

Much

Used with uncountable nouns. It means a large amount.

There isn't much milk left.

How much money do you have?

I don't have much time today.

Grocery Cashier Game

Practice your math skills and use quantifiers in a fun grocery store setting!

Game Background

Grocery Cashier is a math game patterned after cash registers. Your job is to process payments at the checkout as quickly as you can. The catch is that you don't have a barcode scanner, so you'll have to input the price of each item by hand.

How to Play Grocery Cashier

  • Select Tap to Begin to get started. Follow the in-game tutorial to learn the basic functions.
  • To select, left click with your mouse or tap on the screen with your fingers.
  • Type in the value of each item by selecting the numbers on the register.
  • To create decimals, select the decimal point button before pressing a number.
  • Press the plus button to move onto the next item.
  • Select the clear button if you make a mistake.
  • Once you've checked all items, select the total button to get the sum.
  • The customer pays with either bills and coins or a voucher.
  • If the customer gives coins, determine the value using the chart on the right.
  • If the customer gives a voucher, enter the value and press the subtract button.
  • Select Done to finish the transaction.
  • For cash payments, give the correct change.

Basic Gameplay Advice

  • There are two types of transactions: cash and voucher.
  • Voucher transactions are easier and take less time to finish.
  • Cash transactions require processing payment and change, taking slightly longer.
  • You earn points based on how many customers you process and how fast you finish.
  • The game is timed. Finish each checkout before the timer bar reaches the right side.

Practice Using Quantifiers in the Store

As a cashier, you'll need to use quantifiers correctly when talking to customers:

  • "You have a few items today." (countable)
  • "There's a little change from your payment." (uncountable)
  • "We have many customers waiting in line." (countable)
  • "There isn't much time left before closing." (uncountable)
