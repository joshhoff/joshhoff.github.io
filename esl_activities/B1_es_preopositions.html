Understanding Prepositions

A beginner's guide to English prepositions

Guía para hablantes de español

What Are Prepositions?

A preposition is a word that shows the relationship between a noun and other words in a sentence.

Una preposición es una palabra que muestra la relación entre un sustantivo y otras palabras en una oración.

Example:

The book is on the table.

The preposition "on" tells us where the book is located.

How Prepositions Work

Preposition + Noun/Pronoun = Prepositional Phrase

Example:

in + the classroom = in the classroom

He is in the classroom.

The Most Common Prepositions

IN

inside or within something

She is in the house.

ON

touching the surface of something

The pen is on the desk.

AT

a specific point or location

We met at the station.

BY / NEAR

beside or close to something

I sit by the window.

UNDER

below or beneath something

The cat is under the table.

BETWEEN

in the middle of two things

The park is between two buildings.

Prepositions of Location / Posición

These prepositions describe WHERE something is.

IN FRONT OF

The car is in front of the house.

enfrente de

BEHIND

The garden is behind the house.

detrás de

NEXT TO / BESIDE

She sits next to me.

al lado de

ABOVE

The picture is above the sofa.

arriba de / sobre

BELOW

The basement is below the street.

debajo de

Prepositions of Time / Tiempo

These prepositions describe WHEN something happens.

AT

I wake up at 7 a.m.

We meet at noon.

a las (hora específica)

ON

The event is on Monday.

My birthday is on March 5th.

en (día)

IN

I was born in 1990.

See you in five minutes.

en (mes, año, período)

DURING

I studied during the summer.

durante

BEFORE / AFTER

Eat before 9 p.m.

Let's talk after class.

antes de / después de

Prepositions of Direction / Dirección

These prepositions describe which WAY something moves.

TO

I'm going to the park.

She walked to school.

a / hacia

FROM

The bus comes from the city center.

I just arrived from the beach.

de / desde

INTO

Jump into the pool!

He went into the room.

en / dentro de

OUT OF

She walked out of the building.

fuera de / salir de

THROUGH

We walked through the forest.

a través de

ACROSS

They ran across the street.

a través de / cruzar
