Don't vs Not

Learn English negations

🚫

DON'T = DO NOT

In English: Use with action verbs in Present Simple

Українською: Використовуємо з дієсловами дії в Present Simple

Short form of "do not" - to negate actions we do (or don't do) regularly

Скорочення від "do not" - заперечуємо дії, які робимо (або не робимо) регулярно

📌 Formula:

📌 Формула:

I / You / We / They + DON'T + verb

✅ Examples:

✅ Приклади:

  • ✓
    I don't like coffee. — I don't enjoy coffee. — Я не люблю каву.
  • ✓
    They don't speak Ukrainian. — They can't communicate in Ukrainian. — Вони не розмовляють українською.
  • ✓
    We don't eat meat. — We're vegetarians. — Ми не їмо м'ясо.

⚠️ Attention! For he/she/it we use DOESN'T (does not)

⚠️ Увага! Для he/she/it використовуємо DOESN'T (does not)

She doesn't work here. — She doesn't have a job here.

She doesn't work here. — Вона тут не працює.

❌

NOT

In English: Use with TO BE and auxiliary verbs

Українською: Використовуємо з дієсловом TO BE та допоміжними дієсловами

Negation for states, characteristics, and with modal verbs

Заперечення для станів, характеристик та з модальними дієсловами

📌 Using NOT:

📌 Використання NOT:

1. With the verb TO BE:

1. З дієсловом TO BE:

am not / is not (isn't) / are not (aren't)
  • • I am not tired. — I'm not feeling tired.
  • • I am not tired. — Я не втомлений.
  • • She is not (isn't) happy. — She's not feeling happy.
  • • She is not (isn't) happy. — Вона не щаслива.
  • • They are not (aren't) students. — They're not enrolled as students.
  • • They are not (aren't) students. — Вони не студенти.

2. With modal verbs:

2. З модальними дієсловами:

can not (can't) / will not (won't) / should not (shouldn't)
  • • I cannot (can't) swim. — I don't have the ability to swim.
  • • I cannot (can't) swim. — Я не вмію плавати.
  • • You should not (shouldn't) go. — It's not advisable for you to go.
  • • You should not (shouldn't) go. — Тобі не варто йти.
  • • He will not (won't) come. — He won't be attending.
  • • He will not (won't) come. — Він не прийде.

3. With have/has (Present Perfect):

3. З have/has (Present Perfect):

  • • I have not (haven't) seen him. — I haven't encountered him.
  • • I have not (haven't) seen him. — Я його не бачив.
  • • She has not (hasn't) finished. — She hasn't completed it.
  • • She has not (hasn't) finished. — Вона не закінчила.

⚖️ DON'T vs NOT Comparison Порівняння DON'T vs NOT

DON'T NOT (with to be) NOT (з to be)
I don't work. ❌ I am not working. ❌
They don't understand. They are not ready.
We don't have time. It is not important.

💬 Conversation Practice 💬 Розмовні вправи

Practice these dialogues out loud. Pay attention to the use of negatives.

Практикуйте ці діалоги вголос. Зверніть увагу на використання заперечень.

🍽️ Dialogue 1: At a restaurant Діалог 1: В ресторані

Waiter: Офіціант: Would you like some coffee?
You: Ви: No, thank you. I don't drink coffee.
Waiter: Офіціант: How about some dessert?
You: Ви: I'm not hungry anymore, but my friend doesn't have any dessert yet.
💡 Note: "don't drink" — regular action; "not hungry" — state with to be 💡 Зверніть увагу: "don't drink" — звичайна дія; "not hungry" — стан з to be

🏠 Dialogue 2: Weekend plans Діалог 2: Плани на вихідні

Friend: Друг: Are you free on Saturday?
You: Ви: I'm not sure. I don't have any plans yet.
Friend: Друг: Can you come to my party?
You: Ви: I can't promise, but I'll try. I don't want to miss it!
💡 Note: "can't" — modal verb + not; "don't want" — regular action 💡 Зверніть увагу: "can't" — модальне дієслово + not; "don't want" — звичайна дія

💼 Dialogue 3: At work Діалог 3: На роботі

Colleague: Колега: Did you finish the report?
You: Ви: I haven't finished it yet. I didn't have enough time.
Colleague: Колега: Is it difficult?
You: Ви: It's not difficult, I just don't have all the information.
💡 Note: "haven't" — Present Perfect; "didn't" — Past Simple 💡 Зверніть увагу: "haven't" — Present Perfect; "didn't" — Past Simple
