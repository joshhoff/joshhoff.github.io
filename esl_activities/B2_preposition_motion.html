🚀 Prepositions of Movement

Master English prepositions with interactive exercises and conversation practice

INTO
Movement from outside to inside a space or container
• She walked into the room quietly.
• The cat jumped into the box.
• We drove into the tunnel.
OUT OF
Movement from inside to outside a space or container
• He ran out of the building.
• The bird flew out of its cage.
• Water poured out of the bottle.
THROUGH
Movement from one side to the other, passing inside something
• We walked through the forest.
• The train goes through the mountain.
• Light shines through the window.
ACROSS
Movement from one side to the other side of a surface
• She swam across the river.
• The dog ran across the street.
• We walked across the bridge.
ALONG
Movement following the length or direction of something
• They strolled along the beach.
• The car drove along the highway.
• We hiked along the mountain trail.
AROUND
Movement in a circular path or surrounding something
• The children ran around the playground.
• We walked around the lake.
• The satellite orbits around Earth.
UP / DOWN
Movement in a vertical direction (higher/lower)
• She climbed up the stairs.
• The ball rolled down the hill.
• The elevator went up to the top floor.
OVER / UNDER
Movement above or below something
• The plane flew over the city.
• The cat crawled under the table.
• We jumped over the fence.
