English Subject Pronouns

Subject Pronouns

Subject pronouns replace the subject of a sentence.

👤

I

First person singular

Example: I am happy.

👥

We

First person plural

Example: We are friends.

👉

You

Second person (singular/plural)

Example: You are kind.

Third Person Pronouns

These pronouns refer to other people or things.

👨

He

Third person masculine

Example: He is tall.

👩

She

Third person feminine

Example: She is smart.

📦

It

Third person neutral

Example: It is big.

Plural Pronouns

These pronouns refer to groups of people or things.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

They

Third person plural

Example: They are students.

👥

We

First person plural

Example: We are friends.
