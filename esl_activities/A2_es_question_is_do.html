How to Make Questions in Present Tense Cómo Hacer Preguntas en Presente

Using DO/DOES Usando DO/DOES

Use DO/DOES for action verbs and descriptions with "have" Usa DO/DOES para verbos de acción y descripciones con "have"

Structure: Estructura:

DO/DOES + subject + verb + ?

Examples: Ejemplos:

• Do you have brown hair?

• Does she wear glasses?

• Do they have blue eyes?

Using IS/ARE Usando IS/ARE

Use IS/ARE for descriptions with adjectives Usa IS/ARE para descripciones con adjetivos

Structure: Estructura:

IS/ARE + subject + adjective + ?

Examples: Ejemplos:

• Is he tall?

• Are you blonde?

• Is her hair curly?

Quick Reference: Referencia Rápida:

DO: I, you, we, they

DOES: he, she, it

ARE: you, we, they

IS: I, he, she, it
