💡 Quick Tips

  • • Object pronouns usually go before the conjugated verb
  • • With infinitives, you can attach pronouns to the end
  • • "Le" and "les" change to "se" when used with "lo/la/los/las"
  • • Possessive pronouns must agree in gender and number
